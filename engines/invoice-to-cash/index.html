Invoice-to-Cash Engine

The Invoice-to-Cash Engine converts every completed engagement into collected revenue. It generates invoices within 24 hours of a billing trigger, tracks payment against due dates, chases automatically, escalates through a defined ladder, and reconciles the moment payment lands. Gene owns the numbers. Lars owns the deadlines. Lemmy verifies terms. Sade manages client relationships during friction. Pepe fires every invoice. Nothing slips because the engine runs on a cron, not on memory.

Actor Map

Eight actors. GENE owns all numbers. PEPE holds every invoice send gate. LARS owns deadline enforcement. LEMMY verifies every term. SADE manages client relationship during friction. JANIS executes automatically. JAGGER triggers on deal close. BOWIE owns infrastructure.

GENE

CFO. Engine Owner

Generates all invoices. Tracks payment status. Runs the chase cadence. Reconciles receipts within 24h. Produces weekly scorecard every Friday. Owns Invoice Tracker and accounting.db.
LARS

COO. Deadline Enforcement

Flags overdue at day 14. Escalates to Pepe at day 30. Nags daily at day 45+. Tracks billing milestones per EL. Never lets an overdue invoice go silent.
LEMMY

CLO. Terms Gate

Verifies invoice terms match EL exactly before every send. Drafts formal payment demand at day 45. Reviews late payment provisions. Advises on legal options for disputed invoices.
SADE

Client Success. Relationship

Assesses relationship health when an invoice is overdue. Hard push or diplomatic hold? One recommendation to Pepe at day 30. One upfront ETA message, one resolution message. No status loops to clients.
JANIS

System. Automation

Finance filer cron every 4h. Catches payment confirmations, bank alerts, client responses. Triggers reconciliation on detection. Fires Telegram alerts on payment failures within 1h.
JAGGER

CGO. Pipeline Handoff

Fires the billing trigger when a deal closes in the BD pipeline. Ensures billing terms agreed during acquisition match what Gene invoices. No billing term surprises at invoice stage.
BOWIE

CTO. Infrastructure

Finance filer cron health. Budget Bakers API bridge. Email triage finance hooks. On-call when the finance filer fails. Owns connector-health monitoring for all finance integrations.
PEPE

Owner. Fire Away Gate

Sole approver for every invoice send. Also decides on disputed invoices, write-offs, and payment plan negotiations. Billed amount, billing date, and terms are always Showtime.

Engine Run. Automated Sequence

Finance filer runs every 4h via cron. Hard stop: 8 minutes per run. Writes STATE.md on every run regardless of outcome.

Step 1 Load State JANIS

Read finance STATE.md for last_run timestamp. Load all open invoices from Invoice Tracker (status NOT IN reconciled, written_off).

Standing Rules

Applies to every invoice, every client, every billing type.

  • Invoices are Showtime. "Fire Away" are the only words that authorize send. Drafting is Soundcheck. Sending is Showtime. No exceptions.
  • Invoice terms must match the signed engagement letter exactly. Lemmy verifies before every send. Any discrepancy blocks until resolved.
  • Payment failure escalation is immediate. Returned, declined, or bounced payments get Telegram alert within 1h and daily nag until confirmed resolved.
  • No overdue invoice goes silent. Lars flags at day 14. Escalates at day 30. Daily nag at day 45+. The chase never stops until payment or explicit write-off decision from Pepe.
  • All invoices sent from the correct account. Mexzungu clients: pepe@mexzungu.com. NCA: pepe@nobodies.team. Personal account is never used for billing.
  • All invoices are replies in the existing client thread when one exists. Never a fresh cold email. Thread context matters to the client finance team processing payment.
  • Missing receipts are always flagged explicitly in financial reports. Estimated figures without a flag are banned.
  • Reconciliation happens within 24h of payment receipt. Not at end of month. Not when someone remembers. Within 24h, every time.
  • Budget Bakers is read-only. Revenue entries go to accounting.db and Invoice Tracker. Never claim Budget Bakers was updated directly.
  • One Telegram status update per invoice milestone. Sent: one message. Paid: one message. No iterative status loops to Pepe or to clients.
