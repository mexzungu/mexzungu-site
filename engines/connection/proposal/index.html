Cross-Engine

Inputs and outputs.

Proposal Engine talks to one engine: CAE. In from Phase 5.3, out to Phase 5.4.

Inputs
From CAE Phase 5.3 Go decision fires with proposal brief. Payload: contact, company, scope agreed, billing type, fee, jurisdiction, discovery notes, EL template reference.
Outputs
To CAE Phase 5.4 Countersigned EL event. Payload: client ID, signed date, EL Drive link. CAE takes over: CRM to won, Delivery ExCo notified, first invoice scheduled.
