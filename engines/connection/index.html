Signal Routing

How signals move.

Every engine hands its output cleanly to the next. No dead ends, no orphaned leads.

Annie classifies post reactions, comments, and DMs in SM Content. Deal-level signals route to Connection Acquisition as new leads with state=warm.
Joni classifies every comment and DM in SM Engagement. Class C deal signals route to Connection Acquisition immediately, not on daily batch.
Connection Acquisition qualifies, warms, and runs discovery. On Go decision, triggers Proposal Engine with a proposal brief and waits.
Proposal Engine owns the full close: proposal, EL, inline signing, countersign detection. On countersigned EL, fires event back to Connection Acquisition for CRM update and Delivery ExCo handoff.
