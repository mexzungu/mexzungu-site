Engine Scope

What this engine owns
  • Signal detection across all channels
  • Lead qualification and briefing
  • First contact (Showtime, Fire Away required)
  • Relationship warming and D14 cadence
  • Discovery conversations and conflict checks
  • Go / No-Go decision
  • Proposal Engine handoff
  • Countersigned EL receipt and delivery trigger
  • Connection preservation: D30 / D90 / D365
What this engine does NOT own
  • Content creation (SM Content Engine)
  • LinkedIn engagement (SM Engagement Engine)
  • Proposal build and EL signing (Proposal Engine)
  • Client delivery after EL (Delivery Engines)

Pipeline States Reference

Every lead in the CRM holds one of these states. Cadence and gate rules are tied to state.

State Definition Advance gate
new Lead entered, no outreach yet Showtime: first contact (Fire Away)
qualified Reviewed and enrolled, interest not yet confirmed Showtime: first contact (Fire Away)
warm Initial contact made, interest unconfirmed Soundcheck cadence at Day 14
active Two-way dialogue, deal possible Soundcheck cadence at Day 14
proposal Proposal Engine called Soundcheck cadence at Day 7
stale Gone cold, 28+ days no response Showtime revival decision after 30 days
won EL countersigned. Handed to Delivery ExCo. N/A. Phase 6 preservation triggers fire.
lost Explicitly declined or Pepe closed Phase 6 graceful close. Never deleted.

  • Phase 0: WA bridge down. No WhatsApp signals. Unblock: Bowie alert. Run email-only scan.

Cross-Engine Handoffs

Signals this engine receives from upstream engines and what it sends downstream on completion.

Inputs from other engines
From: SM Content Engine (Annie) Signal: deal-level engagement on a published post. Data: commenter name, post context, signal type. Enters Phase 0 queue with state=new, source=sm-content.
From: SM Engagement Engine (Joni) Signal: DM or connection signal classified as Class C. Data: contact name, platform, signal class. Enters Phase 0 queue with state=warm, source=sm-engagement.
From: Investors Delivery Engine Signal: Zone 2 or Zone 3 investor flagged as eligible for CAE enrollment. Data: investor profile, deal review path. Enters qualification queue.
Outputs to other engines
To: Proposal Engine (Prince) Signal: Go decision confirmed. Data: proposal-brief.md path, client name, engagement type, confirmed fee structure. Prince owns full proposal lifecycle from this point.
To: Founders Delivery Engine Signal: EL signed, delivery handoff. Data: client name, scope, practice lead, kickoff date. Triggered via Lars Telegram OPS ping after countersigned EL event received.
