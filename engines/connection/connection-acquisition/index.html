Connection Acquisition Engine

Turns relationships into signed engagements. Runs every morning at 07:00 CET. Owns everything from the moment a signal appears to the moment a countersigned EL lands in Lemmy's registry.

Direction
Jagger
CGO. Sets pipeline targets, sector priorities, cadence rules.
Execution
Joan
Lead generation lead. Owns qualification, discovery, proposal handoff.
Gate
Pepe
Showtime authority. Fire Away on first contact, close, EL send, referral ask.

Top 3 KPIs

2+ ELs signed per month 80%+ qualification to first contact rate Under 30 days first contact to Go decision

What this engine owns

  • Signal detection across every channel (email, WhatsApp personal, LinkedIn DMs, referrals, deal reviews).
  • Lead qualification and pipeline classification.
  • First contact (Showtime, Fire Away only).
  • Relationship warming cadence.
  • Discovery conversations and Go / No-Go decision.
  • Proposal handoff to the Proposal Engine and countersigned EL event receipt.
  • CRM update and Delivery ExCo notification on close.
  • Connection Preservation: D30 referral ask (Showtime), D90 and D365 warm pings (Soundcheck).

What it does NOT own

  • Content creation and publishing. Owned by SM Content Engine (Freddie).
  • Post-publish engagement on all platforms. Owned by SM Engagement Engine (Freddie).
  • Proposal build, EL drafting, inline signing, countersign detection. Owned by Proposal Engine (Prince).
  • Client delivery after EL signed. Owned by Founders or Investors Delivery Engine.
  • Invoice generation and chasing. Owned by Billing Engine (Gene).
Classification

Pipeline states

Nine states. Every lead sits in exactly one. Advancement and cadence rules are the same for every lead in a given state.

StateDefinitionAdvance gateCadence rule
new Lead entered, no outreach yet. Showtime first contact Showtime queue after 3 days.
qualified Reviewed by Joan and Bruce, confirmed by Pepe. First outreach pending. Showtime first contact Showtime immediately.
warm Initial contact made, interest unconfirmed. Soundcheck at Day 14 Auto check-in, update next_action_date +14.

Operating Rhythm

Six phases

Phase 0 fires every morning at 07:00 CET and on every inbound signal event. Phases 1 through 5 are event-triggered. Phase 6 runs as a deferred background loop.

Phase 0 Signal Sweep
Trigger
07:00 CET cron + inbound event on any channel
Owner
Janis execution. Joan classification. Jagger sector priority.
Exit
Signal queue reviewed. Leads created or discarded. Engine run log written.

Sub-tasks

  • 0.1 Bowie WA bridge status check. If degraded, log to run report and proceed with reduced-confidence signals. Never abort the run.
  • 0.2 Scan WhatsApp personal for inbound business signals. Keywords: deal, invest, legal, contract, raise, structure, advisory, founder, cap table, term sheet, introduce, work together. Window: last 24h.
  • 0.3 Scan email (all 4 accounts) for signals. Keywords: partnership, collaboration, proposal, scope, retainer, advisory, counsel, introduction, recommendation. Window: last 24h.
  • 0.4 Check referral signals: scan WhatsApp for "I wanted to introduce," "thought of you," "someone who could help" from existing contacts and clients.
  • 0.5 Check deal review outputs. Zone 2 or Zone 3 investor from last 48h is eligible for enrollment if not already in pipeline.
  • 0.6 Scan existing pipeline for cadence triggers: for each lead, check days_since_last_contact against state threshold. Build action queue.
  • 0.7 Joan classifies each new signal: (A) new lead, (B) update existing, (C) discard. 3-question filter: (1) Does Pepe know them? (2) Is there a plausible engagement? (3) Is this MGL's market? Log every discard reason. Never delete the signal record.
  • 0.8 Cross-check candidates against pipeline and client registry to avoid duplicates. If duplicate: merge into existing record.
  • 0.9 Lemmy conflict check on new candidates: search client registry, active investments, CRM for target company or key person. If conflict: Showtime flag to Pepe before any contact.
  • 0.10 Write engine run log: signals detected, new leads, updated leads, Showtime items queued, cadence actions fired, discards. Path: brain/areas/mexzungu/bd/engine-runs/YYYY-MM-DD.md
Phase 1 Qualification
Trigger
New signal classified as lead candidate in Phase 0
Owner
Joan review. Bruce strategic fit. Jagger pipeline decision.
Exit
Lead discarded with documented reason, or enrolled state=new + Showtime first contact queued.

Sub-tasks

  • 1.1 Joan deep review: research contact on web (background, company, role, recent news). Look for African / LatAm presence, deal stage, funding, governance needs, AI readiness. Never refuse on missing data.
  • 1.2 Check brain/contacts/ and Google Contacts for any prior Pepe interaction with this contact or their company. Surface history to Joan before she writes the brief.
  • 1.3 Bruce strategic fit assessment: MGL positioning fit, deal type within active practice capacity, timing. If capacity constrained: flag to Lars. Do not kill the lead.
  • 1.4 Joan writes qualification brief. 1 page max. Contact background, deal thesis, why now, suggested engagement type, estimated value, risk factors, Pepe relationship distance. Path: brain/areas/mexzungu/bd/quals/YYYY-MM-DD-[slug].md
  • 1.5 Jagger reviews qualification brief. Approves enrollment or marks state=hold with a reason. 30-day review reminder on hold. Never mark as lost prematurely.
  • 1.6 Create lead in CRM: track_lead with all available fields. State = new. Minimum required: name, company, deal_type, contact method, notes.
  • 1.7 Showtime queue: draft first contact message in Pepe's voice. Send to Telegram alerts (thread_id=7) with lead context and proposed message. Wait for Fire Away.
  • 1.8 Log qualification decision to pipeline-log.md: date, lead, enrolled/discarded, reason. Append-only.
Phase 2 First Contact (Showtime)
Trigger
Pepe says "Fire Away" on the queued draft
Owner
Janis execution. Lemmy channel and compliance. Pepe Fire Away.
Exit
First contact sent. State = warm. Acknowledgment window started.

Sub-tasks

  • 2.1 Confirm channel. Default: WhatsApp personal (search_contacts for number). Fallback: email if no WA number known. Never cold email without confirming no WA alternative exists.
  • 2.2 Confirm draft is in Pepe's voice. Rules: warm not sycophantic, specific not generic, references something real (mutual contact, deal, article, event), one clear ask or observation. No pitch. Max 3 sentences. Never present a substandard draft.
  • 2.3 Lemmy compliance check: no regulated advice, no representations about deals, no unintended obligations. If flag: rewrite. Flag type to Pepe in the Showtime note.
  • 2.4 Showtime queue to Telegram thread_id=7. Format: "FIRST CONTACT: [Name] / [Company]. [Channel]. Draft: [message]. Type 'Fire Away' to send." Fire Away is the only trigger.
  • 2.5 Send on Fire Away: send_whatsapp or send_email via build_email_html. Log delivery timestamp. If send fails: retry once. If second failure: Bowie alert.
  • 2.6 Update CRM: state=warm, last_contact=today, next_action_date=+14, notes="First contact sent [DATE] via [channel]."
  • 2.7 Log to pipeline-log.md: first contact sent, channel, date, response window opened.
  • 2.8 Start acknowledgment monitor: internal calendar event only. If no response in 14 days, trigger Phase 3 follow-up cadence. No invite to contact.
Phase 3 Relationship Warming
Trigger
Response received, or 14-day cadence trigger
Owner
Sade health. Janis cadence. Joan value delivery.
Exit
Two-way dialogue confirmed. State = active. Discovery brief drafted.

Sub-tasks

  • 3.1 Response detection: scan WhatsApp and email daily for any reply. On receipt: log immediately and pause cadence timer. Use wa_conversation for full thread context before drafting any reply.
  • 3.2 Classify response tone: positive (interest, question, gratitude), neutral (acknowledged), negative (not interested), no response (cadence continues). Negative advances to Phase 6.
  • 3.3 Draft warm reply (positive or neutral): acknowledge, add value, no pitch yet. Reply in-thread. Reply-all if email. Soundcheck
  • 3.4 Value delivery: if contact has a visible deal, challenge, or initiative in public sources, Joan flags it for Pepe as a brief Soundcheck observation. No ask. Only when genuine. Do not manufacture connection.
  • 3.5 Day-14 cadence (no response): Janis auto-sends check-in via same channel as first contact. Update next_action_date +14. If warm 28+ days: state=stale + Showtime revival alert.
  • 3.6 Sade health monitoring: scan each warm and active lead weekly for response rate, time to reply, warmth of tone. Flag declining temperature. If critical: draft Pepe-direct recovery message for Showtime.
  • 3.7 Discovery trigger: when contact signals a specific need (deal question, scope ask, challenge mentioned), Joan marks discovery-ready and moves to Phase 4. State = active. Do not advance without a confirmed need.
  • 3.8 Update pipeline-log.md at every touchpoint. Append-only.
Phase 4 Discovery + Go Decision
Trigger
State = active. Contact has signaled a specific need.
Owner
Joan brief. Bruce strategic. Pepe discovery call.
Exit
Discovery brief complete. Engagement confirmed. Go / No-Go made.

Sub-tasks

  • 4.1 Joan reads full WhatsApp and email thread to date. No skimming. Extracts: stated problem, deal stage, urgency, stakeholders, prior advisors, budget signals, timeline. Full-read rule: no summary as substitute for source.
  • 4.2 Joan drafts discovery brief. 2 pages max. Problem diagnosis, deal type recommendation, practice lead assignment, suggested scope, value range, key risks, questions for discovery call. Path: brain/areas/mexzungu/bd/discovery/YYYY-MM-DD-[slug].md
  • 4.3 Bruce strategic layer: reviews brief. Adds competitive context, timing signal, scenario stress-test (3 questions: deal falls through, competitor gets there first, client can't pay). Appends to brief file.
  • 4.4 Practice lead assignment. Joan and Bruce agree by deal type: Venture Architecture (Joan), Equity (Tina), Debt (PJ), M&A (Aretha), Asset deals (Chrissie), AI Ops (Kim). Multi-practice: name lead + co-lead.
  • 4.5 Lemmy pre-clearance if new company: 5-step conflict check. (1) Client registry. (2) Active investments. (3) Drive search. (4) CRM. (5) Contacts database. Showtime if any conflict. Do not schedule call until CLEAR.
  • 4.6 Discovery call scheduling per calendar-events.md: full location, full agenda, attendees, prep docs linked. Showtime if new contact. Fire Away before sending invite.
  • 4.7 Pre-call prep to Pepe: Telegram brief max 5 lines. Who they are, what they want, what we think the deal is, what to listen for, suggested scope. Send to main Telegram, not alerts.
  • 4.8 Post-call debrief: 1-page summary written within 4h of call end. Path: brain/areas/mexzungu/bd/discovery/YYYY-MM-DD-[slug]-debrief.md. If not written in 24h: Lars flags Pepe.
  • 4.9 Go / No-Go: Joan + Bruce produce a one-line recommendation. Pepe confirms in Telegram. Soundcheck. No-Go advances to Phase 6. Go advances to Phase 5.
  • 4.10 Update CRM: discovery brief path, practice lead, engagement type, value estimate, Go/No-Go.
Phase 5 Proposal Handoff + Close
Trigger
Go decision confirmed. Scope and engagement type agreed.
Owner
Gene fee. Joan brief. Janis handoff and state management.
Exit
Proposal Engine called. On countersigned EL: CRM won, Delivery ExCo notified, preservation triggers scheduled.

Sub-tasks

  • 5.1 Gene fee confirmation: apply pricing matrix. Confirm fee structure, 3 payment options (upfront / milestone / retainer + success). Bank hours option mandatory. Showtime if non-standard. Pepe sets pricing when matrix cannot resolve.
  • 5.2 Joan writes proposal brief for Proposal Engine: client name, engagement type, scope summary, discovery brief path, practice lead, confirmed fee structure, payment options, timeline, tone notes. Path: brain/areas/mexzungu/bd/proposal-briefs/YYYY-MM-DD-[slug].md
  • 5.3 Trigger Proposal Engine: Joan passes proposal-brief.md path to Prince. CAE enters waiting state. Proposal Engine owns proposal build, EL auto-generation from client selections, DocuSeal inline signing, countersign detection. CAE does not touch the proposal or EL.
  • 5.4 On countersigned EL event: update CRM state=won, last_contact=today. Update clients/registry.md (status: Active). Write "EL fully executed [date]" to pipeline-log.md. Mark contact referral-source-eligible. If event does not arrive within 48h of expected: Lars flags Joan. Joan pings Prince directly.
  • 5.5 Lars triggers delivery handoff: Telegram OPS message. "[Client] EL signed. Scope: [1-liner]. Type: [practice]. Owner: [practice lead]. Kickoff by [date]." Founders Delivery Engine takes over. Soundcheck
  • 5.6 Schedule deferred Phase 6 triggers: internal calendar events for D30 referral ask (Showtime), D90 warm ping (Soundcheck), D365 warm ping (Soundcheck). Also for No-Go contacts from earlier phases. No invites to contact.
Phase 6 Connection Preservation
Trigger
No-Go at any phase, or D30 / D90 / D365 calendar trigger, or state=lost
Owner
Jagger network. Sade health. Joan value.
Exit
Ongoing background loop. No fixed end.

Sub-tasks

  • 6.1 Graceful close message on No-Go or walk: Joan drafts warm, brief. No pitch, no explanation, no apology. Leave the door open. Showtime Fire Away.
  • 6.2 Contact preserved in CRM: state = lost or collab. Notes record why and when. Never delete.
  • 6.3 D30 referral ask (won contacts only, once per engagement): Joan drafts a soft referral ask. "Who else in your network is building something similar where this kind of support would help?" Showtime Fire Away. If declined or ignored: never repeat.
  • 6.4 D90 / D365 warm pings: auto-send lightweight check-in only if a genuine hook exists (news, deal signal, congratulations). No hook: skip that cycle. Do not manufacture connection. Soundcheck
Authority

Showtime gates

Everything else runs autonomously. Only these decisions require Pepe.

Fire Away Rule
First contact. Stale revival. EL send. Day-30 referral ask.

The engine can qualify, warm, monitor, and pass to Proposal without a single Showtime trigger. But four moments always stop at Pepe.

First contact. Every new lead's first message stops in the Telegram alerts thread until Pepe says "Fire Away."

Stale revival. When a warm lead crosses 28 days silent, Janis flags. Pepe decides: kill or revive.

EL send. Proposal Engine owns the flow, but Pepe's Fire Away releases every EL.

Day-30 referral ask. One shot per engagement. Pepe approves the ask or it never fires.
