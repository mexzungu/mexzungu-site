Non-Negotiable

Standing Rules

Applies to every content piece, every channel, every cycle.

  • No em dashes, en dashes, or hyphen-pauses anywhere in content. Periods and commas only.
  • Fire Away and only Fire Away authorizes publish. No other phrase. No approval-by-silence.
  • Social images hosted at mexzungu.com/img/social/ only. api.mexzungu.com is auth-protected and will break silently on external platforms.
  • Never copy-paste the same text across platforms. LinkedIn gets the full story. X gets the sharpest line. Instagram gets the visual moment.
  • Every Outlaw Chronicles issue must have a cover image 1200x627. Never publish text-only to Substack.
  • Instagram grid changed Jan 2025: 1080x1350 portrait only. Not 1080x1080 squares.
  • Content calendar must never run below 3 briefs queued. Lars escalates when it does.
  • After every LinkedIn scrape, auto-draft replies for all unanswered comments. Never wait to be asked.
  • No channel goes dark for more than 7 days without explicit Pepe decision.
  • Content-engine-improver loop runs nightly at 21:30 CET. It auto-patches content-engine.py and logs scores. Do not disable it.
