Jamming Together

Good partnerships start with clarity. This tool models how the economics work across our three collaboration structures, so we can focus the conversation on the work, not the math.

Numbers are illustrative. Every engagement is priced individually after a proper conversation. The formulas are real, and the intent is always a deal that feels right for both sides.

Three ways to work together

Every collaboration is different. The structure depends on your involvement: are you making the connection, bringing the expertise, or working the engagement alongside us?

Referral
One of us makes the connection

Either of us spots the right fit and makes the intro. The referrer steps back, no delivery work required. The other side handles everything, and the referrer earns 10% of Year 1 billings.

10% of Year 1 billings
Advisory
You bring the expertise

You deliver directly to the client under our umbrella. We take care of the infrastructure: contracts, invoicing, admin. You keep most of the upside.

80% to you / 20% studio fee
Co-work
We work on it together

We're both in the room and both doing the work. Fees are distributed based on which services each of us handles. Straightforward, no debate.

Fees split by work distribution
