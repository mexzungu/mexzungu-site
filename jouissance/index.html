Workshop
August 14
What to Expect

A 4-hour immersive experience in somatic and sexual awareness. You will be in a small group of women who have done the same 14 days of preparation you are doing now. What happens in the room depends on what you bring into it.

How to Prepare

Complete your daily rituals. Do the Know Yourself tests. Write honestly in your profile. The more you bring to the app, the more you will bring to the room.

How to Arrive

Come rested. Come without an agenda. Wear something beautiful underneath and something comfortable on top. Bring your yoga mat. Leave your performance at the door.

FAQ
When?+
Saturday, August 14. Doors open at 7pm. Plan for the full evening.
Where?+
Agathe's place. Address is in your invitation message.
What to wear?+
Something beautiful underneath. Something comfortable on top. The layers are part of it.
What to bring?+
Your yoga mat.
What is this, exactly?+
A guided evening of presence, slow burn, and pleasure. Not a class. Not a lecture. An experience designed to let you feel things you usually don't give yourself permission to feel. Come with curiosity and an open body.
Do I need to prepare anything?+
Yes. The daily practices in the app. Five to ten minutes each. Do them. They are not homework. They are how you arrive ready.
Do I need experience with anything?+
No. The only thing required is willingness.
