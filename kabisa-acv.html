Three fights that matter most

1. Longstop (row 1) Not in the signed TS at all. ACV IC introduced a 2yr forced conversion after signing. Push 4yr, settle 3yr. CJ has signalled 3yr is acceptable.
2. Reserved matters thresholds (rows 5-7) TS said "material indebtedness," "material related-party transactions," "material asset disposals" with no numbers. SL adds $250K debt, $25K related-party, $50K asset disposal. Each blocks routine EV infrastructure capex and operations.
3. Commitment period (row 8) TS says founders commit for the 4-year lock-up period. SL ties commitment to "for so long as this SAFE remains outstanding." With a 2yr longstop, ACV could force conversion and end the full-time obligation early.

7 Asset disposal: $50K thresholdSide Letter s.2.1 Stricter than TS High TS said "material asset disposals." SL sets $50K (or 15% of total assets) outside ordinary course. 14 Budget deviation threshold: 15% / $50KSide Letter s.2.1.13 In TS, pushing back High The TS included this threshold but $50K aggregate across all line items in a year will be breached by routine operational variance in an EV infrastructure business. Push: 25% of any individual line item or USD 400,000 in aggregate across all line items per year.Market standard for a high-growth infrastructure company at this stage.
