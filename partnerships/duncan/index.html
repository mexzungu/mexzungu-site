Up to 40% of fees under any workstream may be converted into equity at the price of the next closing round, at the client's election, exercised before invoice. Available across all four practices.

How we work together
Where our work connects.

Duncan works at the capital strategy and investor readiness layer. Mexzungu works at the legal structure and deal close layer. The natural handoff is when a founder has a fundable story and needs the legal architecture to back it up.

  • Founder referral: fundraising track
    Duncan identifies a founder preparing to raise. Mexzungu handles Venture Architecture (WS1) and Capital Closing (WS2). Success fee arrangement structured per engagement, no rigid formula.
  • Mexzungu referral: governance and investor readiness
    Pepe's clients often need board structure, KPI discipline, and investor narrative before the legal work is useful. Duncan's board and commercial advisory expertise is the natural fit there.
  • Joint mandates: Africa and EMEA
    For cross-border mandates where both governance advisory and legal close are needed, a joint scope can be structured from the start. Mexzungu leads legal, Duncan leads capital and governance. One client relationship, shared credit.
  • Portfolio and existing mandates
    If Duncan is already on the board or in a fractional role and a legal need arises, Mexzungu can be brought in as deal counsel or GC without disrupting the existing relationship. Warm handoff, no conflict.
