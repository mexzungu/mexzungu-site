01 · What we do

Two lanes we own.

These are the moments you should think of us. When someone in your network hits one of these two situations, that's the intro. Everything else is optional colour.

01

Complex problem solving.

Cross-border deals stalled on legal. Governance restructures. Cap table cleanup before a raise. IP frameworks for founders who never got round to it. Whatever the last firm called "too complex," we take.

  • Structure memos that turn a founder into an institutional counterparty
  • Capital architecture and instrument selection (SAFE, priced, convertible, revenue-based)
  • Cap table cleanup, secondary rounds, founder equity re-alignment
  • Cross-border entity design (EU, Africa, LatAm, offshore)
  • Governance for impact businesses (purpose trusts, cooperative structures, mission locks)
  • IP frameworks and founder assignment agreements
Venture Architecture bundle. €7,500 flat. Five deliverables. Billed on delivery. No upfront cash. Convertible to equity up to 40% at client election.
02

Closing deals.

The transaction lane. Fundraising rounds, debt facilities, secondaries, M&A, cross-border capital. We run point on the closing, coordinate local counsel where needed, and land the money. Fee is a slice of what actually clears.

  • Equity rounds. Priced or convertible. Any stage.
  • Debt facilities. Venture debt, structured debt, DFI capital.
  • Secondary sales and founder liquidity events
  • M&A on the sell side or the buy side (small to mid-cap)
  • Cross-border structuring for capital moving between EU, Africa, LatAm, GCC
  • SPV and fund structures for co-investment groups
Success fee only. 2% of equity closed. 1% of debt closed. Payable 14 days after funds clear. Nothing until money lands.
02 · Who we do it for

Four people to look for.

Not a taxonomy. Just four concrete profiles you'll recognise in your network. If someone fits one, that's your intro. If it feels close, send it anyway and we qualify from there.

F1

Founder · The Freshly-Funded Founder

Just closed a round. New board. Cap table is a mess and the shareholders agreement is a template someone downloaded. Governance is now their biggest problem and they don't know it yet.

You'll spot them becausethey're posting about "we just raised" and quietly panicking about board mechanics.
F2

Founder · The Impact Builder

Building something mission-locked. Cooperative structure, purpose trust, foundation vehicle. Wants governance that matches the values, not standard VC boilerplate. Traditional counsel doesn't get it.

You'll spot them becausethey use words like "purpose," "community," and "stewardship" the same way other founders use "growth."
I1

Investor · The Fund Placing Its First Bet in a New Geography

Venture fund, family office, or DFI moving into Africa, LatAm, or a GCC market for the first time. Has capital and appetite. Needs local counsel that talks to international counsel, and someone who can hold both sides of the table.

You'll spot them becausethey're asking around for "someone who knows both worlds" and no one has a name.
I2

Investor · The Angel Syndicate Structuring an SPV

Five to fifteen angels pooling into a single deal. Needs the vehicle set up, offering docs drafted, cap table on the target cleaned up, and the closing run. Standard for one person, hairy for a group.

You'll spot them becausea lead angel is WhatsApping around trying to organise everyone, and someone said "we need a lawyer."
Please skip

Big traditional banks (long process, low return). First-time founders with paper-only ideas and no funding (send them once they raise). Pure litigation. Criminal, family, immigration, employment tribunal. Domestic-only work with no international angle. Anyone whose main filter is the cheapest hourly rate.
