Engagement Terms of Service

Version 1.0 — Effective 29 April 2026

These terms apply to every engagement letter issued by Mexzungu Group Ltd. They are incorporated by reference into the engagement letter you signed. If the engagement letter says something different on a specific point, the engagement letter wins.

1. Where we sit

The Studio is a Mauritius company. We deliver from there to wherever the Client happens to be. Pepe signs as Chief Disruption Officer. Nothing in any engagement letter means we have opened a branch, established a presence, or started practising law in the Client's jurisdiction. We are the visiting architects, not the local lawyers.

2. This is not legal advice

What we provide is legal consulting: architecture, risk identification, strategic positioning, document review, and negotiation support. We do not file papers, sign legal opinions, appear in court, or act as the Client's lawyer of record anywhere. Think of us as the friend who has closed a few billion dollars in deals across a bunch of countries and is sitting next to you at the table, telling you what to look out for.

3. What we are not doing

Unless an engagement letter says otherwise, we are not:

  1. Drafting, filing, or executing anything with any court, registry, notary, or government office.
  2. Acting as the Client's lawyer of record in any jurisdiction.
  3. Representing the Client in front of investors, counterparties, or regulators.
  4. Replacing local counsel. For legal execution in any jurisdiction, the Client will need qualified local lawyers. We can point you toward good ones.

4. What we need from you

When we ask for documents — financials, cap table, term sheets, investor materials, corporate docs — we need them within 5 business days. Late inputs shift the clock accordingly. We will tell you when something is affecting our timeline.

5. Liability

Our total liability to the Client, for anything connected to an engagement, is the total cash fees actually paid to the Studio at the time the claim arises. The equity component of any fee is governed separately by the applicable Advisor Share Agreement and is not included in this cap.

The two things we cannot contractually cap are fraud and willful misconduct. We are not planning on either.

6. Confidentiality

What passes between us stays between us. The Client does not share our memos, analysis, positions, or thinking with anyone outside the engagement without our written permission. We do not share the Client's business, cap table, strategy, or investor relationships with anyone outside the engagement.

This works both ways. It does not expire when the engagement does.

Standard carve-outs apply: information that becomes publicly known, information already in the public domain, information the receiving party developed independently, information disclosed by a third party without breach.

7. Portfolio reference

Once an engagement is complete or public, the Studio may refer to the work in general terms — the type of client, the nature of the transaction, the geography — without naming the Client or sharing any confidential detail. We will never identify the Client without written permission. Client preference (name us or keep it anonymous) is noted in the engagement letter.

8. Term and termination

Each engagement runs from its start date until the earlier of:

  1. All workstreams delivered and all fees settled.
  2. 12 months from the engagement letter date.
  3. Either party giving 14 days written notice, for any reason or no reason at all.

If the engagement ends early: all work delivered to termination date stays delivered; all fees earned to termination date are due; all equity vested to termination date is retained. Post-termination success fees apply to any qualifying closing within 6 months of termination where the Studio introduced or materially supported the transaction during the engagement.

Confidentiality, portfolio reference rights, and post-termination fee provisions survive termination.

9. Governing law

The laws of the Republic of Mauritius govern every engagement. Any dispute that cannot be resolved in good faith between the parties goes to the Supreme Court of Mauritius (Commercial Division), which has exclusive jurisdiction.

We do not expect to need this clause. It is here so neither party ever has to wonder.
