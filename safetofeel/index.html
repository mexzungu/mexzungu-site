About Einat

The work found her.

Einat never planned to become a healer. She travelled to India to heal herself and ended up spending many years in Asia, learning, unlearning, exploring, and travelling alongside other healers. Somewhere along the way, the work found her.

Today, she helps people access what is happening beneath the surface and recognise the patterns that keep creating the same reactions, relationships, and situations.

Her work is intuitive, direct, and deeply compassionate. She has a natural ability to sense what lies beneath the surface and gently bring it into awareness. There is space for everything a person feels, so that what has been held in the body can begin to soften and release.

Through Safe to Feel, Einat creates a space where people feel safe enough to be honest, see what is really there, and let go of what they no longer need to carry. From there, they can move forward with greater clarity, freedom, and trust in themselves.

Offerings

Work with Me

Two ways to begin, shaped around where you are and what you are ready for.

01

Safe to Feel Session

A private online session for people who feel stuck, emotionally overwhelmed, or caught in a pattern they are ready to understand more deeply.

Einat helps you access what may be operating beneath the surface, including subconscious patterns, emotional imprints, and what the body has been carrying.

Each session is intuitive and different. It may include conversation, somatic awareness, energy work, guided inner journeys, emotional release, or simply making space for something not yet fully seen or felt.

The experience includes 60 to 90 minutes, followed by one hour of guided integration rest in the comfort of your own space.

€120
Online
02

The Safe to Feel Journey

A tailor-made three-month private journey for people who are ready to go deeper. Highly personalised, created around your individual needs, rhythm, and inner journey.

Over twelve weeks, Einat works closely with you to uncover the subconscious patterns and emotional imprints shaping your experience. The conditions for what has been held in the body to soften, release, and transform.

  • Six to ten private sessions of up to 90 minutes
  • Personalised rhythm throughout three months
  • Tailor-made practices and reflections
  • Private voice message support throughout
  • Optional Enneagram-based exploration
  • Somatic and emotional awareness practices
  • A closing integration session
  • A personal map for moving forward
Three-month online or in-person private journey
