Background

The Situation

The problem: Approximately 30 Assistant Teachers and Educare Fellows are currently working under employment contracts that treat them as Duara employees. This creates employment law liability under Kenyan law and misrepresents the actual operating model. The School Entrepreneur model requires independent contractor relationships — not employer-employee relationships — to function legally and sustainably.

The fix: A structured 5-phase transition to correct contract types for all three staff tiers, with proper documentation, a comms strategy that preserves relationships, and a withholding tax regime that is compliant from day one.

Legal

Open Items

OI-01
Withholding tax mechanics: 5% WHT applies to payments to Kenyan-resident consultants under the Income Tax Act Cap. 470. Confirm SE is registered as a withholding agent and will deduct and remit. AT and EC fees should be quoted gross.
OI-02
Unsigned prior contracts: Approximately 14 ATs may have unsigned employment contracts in circulation. These do not create binding obligations but should be formally cancelled in writing before the new agreements are issued.
OI-03
Dagoretti situation: The Dagoretti SE abandonment involved a safeguarding incident. Any AT at that school needs careful handling — flag to Pepe before contact. Decision memo prepared separately.
OI-04
Departed ATs with potential claims: Any AT who departed without a signed contract and received payment may have a quasi-employment argument. These must be identified in Phase 1 audit and handled with a release letter before the transition is marked closed.
Staff Profiles

School Entrepreneurs

🚀
SE · School Entrepreneur
The Network Owner
Who they are
The SE is the core operator in the Duara model. They identify, open, and run a local school — typically in a community they are embedded in. They are not employees of Duara. They are independent entrepreneurs who license the Duara brand, curriculum, and support infrastructure under a formal agreement.
What they do
Recruit students and families. Hire and manage Assistant Teachers. Run day-to-day school operations. Pay AT fees from school revenue. Report to Duara on enrolment, quality, and finance. Uphold Duara standards and child safeguarding protocols.
Contract Type Signed / In use
Independent Contractor Agreement (ICA)
Duara → SE direct. The SE is a franchisee / operator, not an employee. The ICA covers brand licence, operating standards, revenue sharing, reporting obligations, and termination. Governing law: Kenya.

Why this structure: The SE takes entrepreneurial risk. They are not controlled at a level that creates employment. They can work for multiple principals. The ICA protects both parties — Duara avoids employment liability; the SE gets a clear commercial relationship with defined IP rights.

Staff Profiles

Assistant Teachers

📚
AT · Assistant Teacher
The Classroom Operator
Who they are
ATs are the front-line teachers working in Duara partner schools. They are community members — often young graduates or experienced local educators — recruited by the SE. They are not Duara employees. They are independent consultants contracted by Duara directly under the new model.
What they do
Deliver the Duara curriculum to students. Track attendance and progress. Report to the SE on a daily/weekly basis. Attend training sessions organised by Duara. Provide invoices or fee notes as required for payment processing.
Contract Type v1.0 — Under review
Consulting Agreement (AT)
Duara → AT direct. The AT is engaged as an independent consultant by Duara Education. The SE is the day-to-day operational supervisor but is not the contractual party — this keeps Duara in control of the legal relationship and compliance obligations.

Why this structure: Previous employment contracts created statutory obligations (notice periods, redundancy, maternity rights, NSSF/NHIF contributions) that Duara cannot sustainably manage across 30+ schools. The consulting model is appropriate because ATs typically work part-time, serve specific engagements, and retain the right to work elsewhere.
Structure confirmed — 11 May 2026

Confirmed: Duara contracts the AT directly (Duara→AT, not SE→AT subcontract). Employment contracts previously in circulation were an administrative error — these are being systematically replaced.

Educare Fellows

🌱
EC · Educare Fellow
The Fellowship Participant
Who they are
Educare Fellows are participants in the Duara Educare programme — a structured fellowship designed to develop early-childhood education practitioners in the communities Duara serves. They are not employees. They receive a fellowship stipend, training, and certification in exchange for their participation and contributions.
What they do
Complete structured training modules in early childhood education. Work alongside SEs and ATs in partner schools as part of their practical placement. Contribute to curriculum delivery at the early childhood level. Receive certification upon programme completion.
Contract Type v1.0 — Under review
Fellowship Agreement (Educare)
Duara → EC direct. The agreement is structured as a fellowship grant — not an employment contract and not a consulting agreement. The EC receives a stipend (not a wage), is enrolled in a defined programme, and has learning obligations alongside any operational contributions.

Why this structure: Fellowship status correctly describes the relationship — Duara is investing in the EC's development, not purchasing labour. This avoids employment law entirely and creates a positive, development-oriented framing that reflects the actual purpose of the Educare programme.

Communications

Comms Guide

  • ✓Frame all outreach as "formalising" the existing relationship — not changing it. The work is the same. The paper is just catching up.
  • ✓Always let the SE brief the AT first before formal contact. The SE is the trusted local relationship.
  • ✓Never use the word "employment" or "employee" in any outreach. Use "partner," "teaching partner," "consulting relationship."
  • ✓If an AT asks "am I an employee?" — the answer is: "No, this is a consulting arrangement, which is standard for the type of work you do with us." Do not elaborate. Flag to Pepe if they push.
  • ✓Keep all correspondence in writing (WhatsApp is fine). It creates a record without being intimidating.
  • ✓Any AT who refuses to sign should be flagged to Pepe immediately — do not negotiate individually or offer modifications to the agreement.
  • ✓Departed ATs: do not contact unless specifically instructed by Pepe. The audit identifies them — the decision on whether to reach out sits with Pepe.
