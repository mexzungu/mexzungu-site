The situation

The problem is fixable — because Duara never signed the wrong contracts.

Around 12 Assistant Teachers and educadores have employment contracts that were signed by the School Entrepreneur only — not by any Duara hub representative — and were never registered with Kenyan tax authorities.

Those contracts are structurally wrong in two ways: they use an employment format when a consulting agreement is the correct structure, and the signing authority is wrong (the SE, not Duara). Because Duara never signed, there is no executed employment relationship to unwind. The gap in the documentation is the problem — and it is also what makes a clean correction possible.

The objective is to withdraw the incorrect documents as an administrative error and replace them with correctly structured consulting agreements before any of the following creates a problem: a tax authority inquiry, a departing AT raising a claim, or a future audit.

Why this is correctable

An employment contract signed only by the SE — not by Duara — has not been executed as between the AT and Duara. The legal exposure from the unsigned contracts is limited. This is an administrative gap, not a binding employment relationship. The window to correct it cleanly is now.

Phase 5 — Open item
Withholding tax: the position from the call needs to be revisited.
Correction to May 7 position

The call assumed withholding tax would not apply to local Kenyan contractors. That is not correct. Under the Income Tax Act Cap. 470, Duara as the payer is required to deduct 5% withholding tax at source on payments to resident consultants and remit it directly to KRA. This is not PAYE — it is a payer obligation on Duara.

Open items on withholding tax:

  • Daphne to confirm with the Duara accountant whether 5% has been applied to any existing consultant payments and what the gap might be.
  • Victoria's contact (free legal review offer) to validate the withholding position before the new consulting agreements are executed at scale.
  • If 5% has not been applied historically, a voluntary disclosure or prospective compliance approach may be the cleanest path — local lawyer to advise.

The new consulting agreement templates (below) already incorporate the 5% withholding deduction clause. No change needed there — just confirmation that it will be applied from the first new payment.

Comms strategy

Neutral tone. No jargon. Not their problem.

The message to ATs and educadores needs to be simple and neutral. This is an administrative correction — not a reclassification, not a cost-cutting exercise, not a signal that their relationship with Duara is changing. The framing should make clear that their day-to-day work is unaffected and that Duara is just fixing the paperwork.

4. What is this agreement?

This agreement sets the base relationship between you and Duara. It allows you to work within the Duara system, build your capacity as a School Entrepreneur, and take real responsibility from day one. This is a foundational contract. Later agreements may add rights, but they do not replace this agreement unless stated clearly.

5. What is this agreement NOT?

This agreement is not an employment contract, a partnership or joint venture, a loan or credit arrangement, a grant or promise of equity, or a guarantee of future operating rights. You never owe money personally to Duara under this agreement.

6. What are you responsible for?

  • Taking ownership of your work, decisions, and conduct
  • Engaging fully with training, planning, and execution activities
  • Following Duara's safeguarding, conduct, and quality standards
  • Managing time, commitments, and communication responsibly
  • Reporting progress, risks, and challenges honestly and on time

7. What does Duara provide?

Duara may provide training, coaching, and mentorship; access to tools, systems, and templates; guidance on school development; and logistical or financial support at its discretion. Any support provided is not a loan and creates no repayment obligations.

8. How does money work (no debt)?

Any money deployed by Duara is at the school or system level, not given to you personally, and does not create personal repayment obligations. If no recovery occurs at the school level, Duara bears that loss.

9. How are fees invoiced and paid?

Fees will be paid based on an invoice or payment record generated by Duara. Invoices must include your full legal name, invoice date, reference period, and agreed monthly fee. Duara will pay valid invoices within 14 days. You are responsible for your own taxes, statutory contributions, and filings.

10. What is my status as an independent contractor?

You act as an independent contractor, not an employee. Nothing in this agreement creates employment rights, paid leave, severance, or statutory employee protections. You control how you perform your work, subject to Duara's standards.

11. Child safety and health & safety

You agree to comply fully with Duara's child safeguarding and protection policies, complete required safeguarding training, report any safeguarding concerns immediately, and act at all times to protect learners' safety. Any breach of safeguarding obligations may result in immediate termination.

12. Confidentiality and data protection

You must keep confidential all learner and family data, internal information, and financial and operational information. These obligations continue after this agreement ends. On exit, all Duara data and materials must be returned or deleted.

13. What happens if this agreement ends?

Either party may end this agreement with 60 days' written notice. During the notice period, you must support handover and continuity. All Duara-owned assets, data, and materials remain with Duara. Duara may terminate immediately for breach of safeguarding, dishonesty, fraud, or serious violation of the code of conduct.

14. Who owns the work and materials?

All curriculum, systems, processes, branding, tools, and materials are and remain Duara's intellectual property, licensed to you only for Duara-related work. Any work created in the course of this role belongs to Duara.

15. Final provisions

Changes must be agreed in writing. If any clause is invalid, the rest still applies. This agreement sits beneath later agreements that grant additional rights.

4. What is this agreement?

This agreement sets out the consulting relationship between you and Duara. You will support learning and school operations at the school named above, as an independent consultant. You are not an employee of Duara.

5. What are you responsible for?

  • Delivering teaching and learning support in line with Duara's curriculum and model
  • Supporting the School Entrepreneur in day-to-day classroom operations
  • Engaging with learners, parents, and the school community in line with Duara's standards
  • Completing attendance, progress, and incident records as required
  • Participating in training and sessions provided by Duara
  • Following Duara's safeguarding, conduct, and quality standards at all times

You report to the School Entrepreneur at your school, and to Duara's operations team where required.

6. What does Duara provide?

Duara may provide access to Duara's curriculum and learning materials, training and coaching sessions, and guidance from Duara's operations team. Any support provided is not a loan and creates no repayment obligations.

7. What is my status as a consultant?

You are an independent consultant, not an employee. Nothing in this agreement creates employment rights, paid leave, severance, or statutory employee protections under the Employment Act. You are free to provide consulting services to other clients during this engagement, as long as this does not conflict with your obligations here.

8. How is my consulting fee paid?

Payment will be made on or before the 5th day of the following month by M-Pesa or bank transfer. Duara will deduct withholding tax at 5% at source, as required under the Income Tax Act Cap. 470 for resident consultants, and remit this directly to KRA. The net amount you receive will reflect this deduction. You are responsible for any other personal tax obligations. You are not entitled to pension contributions, medical cover, leave pay, or any other employment benefit from Duara.

9. Who owns the work and materials?

All materials, lesson records, and content created in the course of this engagement belong to Duara. You may not copy, reproduce, or use Duara's curriculum or materials for any purpose outside this engagement.

10. Child safety

You agree to comply fully with Duara's Safeguarding and Child Protection Policy at all times, complete any required safeguarding training, and report immediately any safeguarding concern, incident, or allegation to the School Entrepreneur and to Duara's operations team. Any breach of safeguarding obligations is considered serious misconduct and may result in immediate termination.

11. What happens if this agreement ends?

Either party may end this agreement with 30 days' written notice by email, SMS, or written letter. Duara may end this agreement immediately, without notice, for breach of safeguarding, dishonesty, fraud, conduct that puts learners at risk, or serious violation of Duara's standards. When this agreement ends, you must stop using Duara's name, materials, and systems; all Duara materials must be returned; fees owed up to the end date will be paid within 14 days. This agreement supersedes any prior arrangement, letter, or contract between us, including any prior employment contract.

12. Confidentiality

You must keep confidential all information about Duara's operations, learners, families, and school community that comes to you through this engagement. This obligation continues for 12 months after this agreement ends.

4. What is this agreement?

This agreement establishes your fellowship with Duara at the school named above. The Educare Fellowship is a structured learning and contribution programme. You will support school operations and gain hands-on experience in community education delivery. This is not an employment contract. You are a Fellow, not an employee or independent contractor.

5. What are you expected to do?

  • Support the School Entrepreneur and learners with educational activities in line with Duara's model
  • Follow the direction and schedule set by the School Entrepreneur and Duara's operations team
  • Participate in Duara training and learning sessions as required
  • Keep records and reports as directed
  • Contribute to a positive and safe learning environment
  • Follow Duara's standards, policies, and safeguarding requirements at all times

6. What does Duara provide?

Duara will provide a structured fellowship experience at a Duara school, supervision and coaching from the School Entrepreneur and Duara's team, and access to Duara's training materials and learning sessions.

7. How is my stipend paid?

Payment will be made on or before the 5th day of the following month by M-Pesa or bank transfer. You do not need to submit an invoice — Duara will process the payment based on your participation record. The stipend is not a salary and does not carry employment benefits. Duara will deduct any withholding tax required by Kenyan law at source and remit it to KRA.

8. What is my status as a Fellow?

You are a Fellow, not an employee of Duara. Nothing in this agreement creates employment rights, paid leave, severance, medical cover, or any other employment benefit. Duara and the School Entrepreneur will give you day-to-day direction. This is a normal part of a structured fellowship and does not create an employment relationship.

9. Child safety

You agree to comply fully with Duara's Safeguarding and Child Protection Policy, complete required safeguarding training before working with learners, and report immediately any safeguarding concern, incident, or allegation. Any breach will result in immediate termination of the Fellowship.

10. What happens if this Fellowship ends?

The Fellowship ends on the end date in Section 3, or earlier if either party gives 14 days' written notice by email, SMS, or written letter. Duara may end the Fellowship immediately for breach of safeguarding, conduct that puts learners at risk, fraud, or serious misconduct. When the Fellowship ends, all Duara materials must be returned and any unpaid stipend owed up to the end date will be paid within 14 days.

11. Materials and confidentiality

All content, records, and materials created during the Fellowship belong to Duara. You must keep confidential all information about Duara's operations, learners, and school community. This obligation continues for 6 months after the Fellowship ends.

12. Final provisions

Changes must be agreed in writing. If any clause is invalid, the rest still applies. This agreement supersedes any prior arrangement or contract between us. Governing law: Kenya.
