Ayembe Loan Restructuring Proposal

A clean slate. A fixed rate. You pick the pace.

Prepared for Alex Kalanda / Ayembe. July 2026.

The terms, in one page

Principal stays. Interest converts. Fixed rate.

The original 2021 loan is being restructured cleanly. The principal stays at USD 35,000 and carries a single fixed rate of 10% p.a., compounding quarterly. All accrued interest from 2021 to today converts into a SAFE that only pays out if you close a priced equity round in any Ayembe business over the next ten years. Repayment starts October 1, 2026. No early repayment penalties.

  • Principal: USD 35,000, repaid in equal quarterly instalments over a term you choose (between 4 and 40 quarters).
  • Fixed rate: 10% per annum, compounding quarterly. One rate. No tiers.
  • SAFE for accrued interest: USD 23,570 converts at a 25% discount at the next priced equity round in any business you control, valid for 10 years.
  • No early repayment penalties: pay down faster whenever cash flow allows, with 5 days notice.
  • First payment: October 1, 2026, then quarterly on the 1st.
  • Governing law: Mauritius. MARC arbitration.
SAFE
Accrued interest becomes equity upside
All interest accumulated since June 2021 is forgiven as a cash obligation and converted into a Simple Agreement for Future Equity. If Ayembe Talent Solutions or any business you control raises a priced round, this SAFE converts at a 25% discount. If no round happens within 10 years, the SAFE expires and the amount is forgiven outright.
