Mexzungu Group: Engagement Proposal

Building Rwanda's first electric cargo fleet deserves legal infrastructure to match.

Prepared for Elyse Byiringiro / BYR Mobility. July 2026. Valid 30 days.

The Situation

The market timing is rare. The legal foundation has to match it.

Elyse, you are moving into a genuinely open market. Rwanda has banned new fuel-powered motorcycles for public transport, tax exemptions on EV imports run until 2028, and no electric operator is currently serving the heavy-duty cargo segment. The petrol trike operators running LIFAN and Zongshen are your actual competition, and they have no electrification pathway.

What you need right now is not a VC pitch. It is a clean legal entity, a defensible cap table, and a fundraising strategy built for the reality of the Rwanda EV market, not a Silicon Valley playbook. You need to know what to offer the first investor, how to protect your data rights from day one, and which capital sources actually fund early-stage mobility in East Africa. That is what this engagement delivers.

Workstream 1 deliverables

  • Rwanda entity setup: Incorporation of BYR Mobility Ltd in Rwanda, articles of association, registered office, directorship documentation, RDB registration.
  • Cap table architecture: Founder share allocation, vesting schedule, option pool sizing for future employees and advisors, pre-money structure ready for the first SAFE or priced round.
  • Shareholder agreement: Founder rights, drag-along and tag-along provisions, right of first refusal, anti-dilution framework, consent matters list.
  • IP assignment agreement: All current and future project IP (technology, brand, data, designs) formally assigned from founders to the company entity.
  • Data rights framework: Sensor data ownership structure, data monetization architecture, third-party data sharing terms. This is a moat. Built in from day one.
  • Governance framework: Board composition and decision rights for the seed stage, consent thresholds, annual meeting requirements, officer roles.

Workstream 2 deliverables

  • Fundraising strategy memo: How much to raise, in what structure (SAFE vs priced round vs grant vs DFI), from whom, and in what sequence for the Rwanda EV market.
  • Capital source targeting list: Curated list of angels, impact funds, DFIs (including BRD climate finance lines and Development Bank of Rwanda access), and sector-specific VCs active in East Africa EV.
  • Pitch materials review and coaching: Your current pitch deck is well-researched. We refine the narrative, sharpen the ask, and integrate the data monetization layer identified in WS1.
  • Term sheet review and negotiation advisory: When an investor comes with terms, we read it, tell you what is standard, what is not, and what to push back on. Rwanda jurisdiction calibration included.
  • Closing coordination: SAFE or priced round documentation, investor onboarding agreements, closing checklist, executed documents filed and stored.

Scope Boundaries

Scope clarity protects both sides.

  • We will not provide Rwandan domestic legal advice. Pepe Carrillo is not licensed to practice law in Rwanda. This engagement provides deal structuring, governance design, and fundraising advisory. For local filings and regulatory submissions, we work alongside a Rwandan law firm.
  • We will not engage directly with Rwanda Revenue Authority, RDB, or other government bodies on your behalf without separate written authorization.
  • We will not make investor introductions without your prior approval of each target. You control who gets access to your company.
