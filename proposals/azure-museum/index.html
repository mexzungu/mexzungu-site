  • Delaware Public Benefit Corporation registration. PBC structure legally protects the social mission while keeping you investor-ready. We coordinate with a Delaware registered agent. Filing fees billed at cost, no markup.
  • Rwanda operating company setup. Local entity for staff, programs, and on-the-ground operations. We coordinate with Kigali-based corporate counsel. Rwanda counsel fees billed at cost, no markup.
  • Holding structure design. How Delaware PBC owns or operates through the Rwanda entity. Clean, documented, and built to withstand investor due diligence.
  • Board governance framework. Board bylaws, board charter, and committee structure. Designed for a small mission-driven board with room to grow as the project scales.
  • IP assignment agreement. Every piece of content, technology, brand asset, and methodology formally assigned from you personally to the entity. Non-negotiable for any serious investor.
  • Cap table design. Founder shares, advisory pool (10-15%), and fundraising headroom. Clean and unambiguous from day one.
  • Fundraising-ready documentation package. One-page entity summary written for the investor conversation. Covers structure, mission lock, governance, and IP status. The questions they will ask before they ask them.
