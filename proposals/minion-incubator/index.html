01. Situation

What We Heard

Minion Incubator Ltd is building a structured early-stage ecosystem in Kenya, with strong deal flow, a growing cohort model, and real traction. The governance layer has not kept pace with that growth.

No formal board structure

Decision-making is informal. Accountability gaps emerge as the portfolio grows and external investors begin asking harder questions.

Inconsistent founder agreements

Each portfolio company has different terms, with no standard framework across IP ownership, equity vesting, or exit mechanics.

Regulatory blind spots

Operating across multiple startup stages and sectors without systematic regulatory tracking for portfolio companies creates exposure.

Scaling faster than structure

Cohort growth is outpacing documentation. Without replicable frameworks, each new intake creates ad hoc legal and governance overhead.

Our Read

Minion Incubator does not have a governance crisis. It has a governance gap that will become a crisis as it scales. The right move: close the gap now, with structure proportionate to current size but designed to handle the next two growth stages without rebuilding from scratch.
