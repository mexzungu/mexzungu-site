02 / What breaks
Four risks. Named and concrete.
1
Studio-built companies carry governance exposure.
Five of your 16 companies were built inside the studio. Board composition, conflict provisions, founder IP not formally assigned. A Series A investor finds this in 30 minutes of DD.
2
30 markets, no common legal standard.
Each market adds regulatory surface area. Without a standard approach to data, employment, and operating agreements, every new portfolio company solves the same problem from scratch.
3
DFI LP compliance is a gating condition, not a future concern.
FMO and GIZ require GDPR-equivalent data protection from portfolio companies before releasing follow-on capital. Most of your 16 companies are not ready.
4
No standard architecture means every round starts from scratch.
No template instruments, no consistent governance, no reusable structure. The cost compounds with every new raise, every new market, every new portfolio company.

The window is the next portfolio company fundraising round. That is when this becomes visible to institutional investors.
