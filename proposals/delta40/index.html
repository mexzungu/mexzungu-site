02 — The Challenge

Delta40 closed a $20M fund in February 2026. Sixteen portfolio companies. Thirty-plus African countries. No in-house legal function.

The complexity runs at two levels simultaneously. At fund level: LP obligations, CMA and CBK regulatory requirements, investor reporting, co-investment structuring, and constitutional documents that govern how capital moves. At portfolio level: entity setup across multiple jurisdictions, commercial contracts, co-founder and employment agreements, IP protection, and fundraising documentation for companies that cannot afford standalone senior legal counsel at the $100k–$500k check stage.

When legal infrastructure breaks at this scale, it does not break quietly. It breaks at due diligence, at fundraising, at regulatory inspection. A Studio Foundation Retainer replaces that exposure with one monthly fee and a legal function that operates across both levels, scales automatically with the portfolio, and does not require Delta40 to manage separate counsel relationships for every jurisdiction or transaction.

05 — Local Counsel Network

Mexzungu's local counsel network covers 30+ African jurisdictions. For matters requiring formal legal opinions, court filings, or in-jurisdiction appearances, Mexzungu coordinates directly with trusted local counsel, briefing them in context and managing the relationship so Delta40 does not have to.

No separate retainer needed for local counsel coordination. Delta40 pays local counsel fees directly only where formal filings or court appearances are required. All coordination, briefing, and quality control is included in the retainer.

Most legal questions across a 30-country portfolio can be answered without triggering local counsel. When local counsel is required, Mexzungu handles the brief, the context, and the oversight. One relationship, across all the markets Delta40 operates in.
