02 — The Challenge

Delta40 closed a $20M fund in February 2026. Sixteen portfolio companies. Thirty-plus African countries. No in-house legal function.

The complexity runs at two levels simultaneously. At fund level: LP obligations, CMA and CBK regulatory requirements, investor reporting, co-investment structuring, and constitutional documents that govern how capital moves. At portfolio level: entity setup across multiple jurisdictions, commercial contracts, co-founder and employment agreements, IP protection, and fundraising documentation for companies that cannot afford standalone senior legal counsel at the $100k–$500k check stage.

When legal infrastructure breaks at this scale, it does not break quietly. It breaks at due diligence, at fundraising, at regulatory inspection. A Studio Foundation Retainer replaces that exposure with one monthly fee and a legal function that operates across both levels, scales automatically with the portfolio, and does not require Delta40 to manage separate counsel relationships for every jurisdiction or transaction.

03 — Scope of Services

Two levels of coverage under a single retainer. Fund-level work and portfolio-level work are both included. No internal allocation decisions, no tracking which company used which hours. That is Mexzungu's problem to manage, not Delta40's.

Fund-Level
  • LP agreement reviews and investor correspondence
  • Fund governance: investment committee support, board resolutions, manager obligations
  • CMA and CBK regulatory compliance monitoring and filing coordination
  • Investor reporting frameworks and disclosure obligations
  • Constitutional documents: fund formation documents, amendments, co-investor frameworks
  • Follow-on round structuring and co-investor negotiations
  • General legal access for Delta40 partners: async unlimited within reasonable scope, calls within 72 hours
Portfolio-Level
  • Entity formation and corporate structuring for new and existing portfolio companies
  • Commercial contracts, term sheets, vendor and supplier agreements
  • Co-founder and employment agreements, equity vesting, and option plans
  • IP registration, assignment, and protection coordination
  • Fundraising documentation per portfolio company: SAFEs, equity rounds, convertible notes, debt facilities
  • Cross-border compliance queries and regulatory navigation across operating jurisdictions

Work outside retainer scope is billed at the preferred overage rate. See engagement model below.

04 — Engagement Model

The Studio Foundation Retainer is a single monthly fee that scales automatically with the size of Delta40's portfolio. No renegotiation at any threshold. One invoice, one relationship, one point of contact across fund and portfolio.

Portfolio size Monthly retainer Coverage
Up to 20 companies Current $5,500/mo Fund-level + full portfolio coverage
21–35 companies $7,000/mo Fund-level + full portfolio coverage
36+ companies $8,500/mo Fund-level + full portfolio coverage

Current portfolio: 16 companies. Starting rate: $5,500/mo. Threshold repricing is automatic and requires no renegotiation.

Equity Option

0.25% equity per new portfolio company onboarded, in lieu of one-time setup fees per company. When Delta40 brings in a new portfolio company and Mexzungu provides the initial legal setup (entity formation, co-founder agreement, corporate structure), the fee can be settled as 0.25% equity in that company at the most recent closing price instead of a cash invoice. This option is elected in writing before work commences on that company.

Overage Rate

Work outside retainer scope including specialized regulatory filings, contested matters, and complex multi-party transactions is billed at $150/hr preferred rate, versus $350+ per hour market rate for comparable cross-border legal advisory.

Success Fee

0.75% of capital raised on any raise above $2M. Not applicable to raises below $2M. Applies to equity rounds, debt facilities, and SAFEs above the threshold. Payable 14 days after capital clears.

Fees are exclusive of registration fees and local counsel fees (if any). Where formal filings or local counsel appearances are required, those costs are quoted separately and passed through at cost.
05 — Local Counsel Network

Mexzungu's local counsel network covers 30+ African jurisdictions. For matters requiring formal legal opinions, court filings, or in-jurisdiction appearances, Mexzungu coordinates directly with trusted local counsel, briefing them in context and managing the relationship so Delta40 does not have to.

No separate retainer needed for local counsel coordination. Delta40 pays local counsel fees directly only where formal filings or court appearances are required. All coordination, briefing, and quality control is included in the retainer.

Most legal questions across a 30-country portfolio can be answered without triggering local counsel. When local counsel is required, Mexzungu handles the brief, the context, and the oversight. One relationship, across all the markets Delta40 operates in.
