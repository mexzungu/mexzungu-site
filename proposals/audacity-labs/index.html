01 / What we heard
The device works. The structure is not ready.

You built something real. EV drivers in Ghana are charging from domestic plugs not designed for the load, and the fire risk is real and growing fast as adoption accelerates. Your smart monitor reads voltage, current, and temperature and disconnects before something burns. The Ghana Energy Commission is paying attention. There is a market in Rwanda too.

The problem is not the device. It is that Audacity Labs is the wrong vessel for what this is becoming. The IP is tied to an entity that was not built for hardware, investors, or cross-border capital. You need a clean new structure: a new entity that holds the IP properly, a cap table that makes sense before the first institutional cheque lands, and fundraising instruments designed for how you are actually going to raise.

That is what we are building.

02 / What we will build
Five deliverables. One clean structure.

Each document is decision-ready. Not a framework, not a deck. Something you can hand to an investor or a government counterpart and have it hold up.

1
Entity Architecture Memo
New HoldCo structure, jurisdiction recommendation (Delaware, European, or African holding entity depending on your investor pipeline), board composition, and decision rights. The blueprint for everything that follows.
2
IP Assignment Agreement
Formal transfer of all IP from Audacity Labs to the new entity. Covers the Goliath engineering contract, the monitoring software, brand, and any patents in process. Clean chain of title before you approach any investor.
3
Cap Table Design
Clean founding structure for the new entity. Founder shares, vesting schedule, option pool if needed. Built to survive a term sheet without surprises on either side.
4
Fundraising Instrument Design
Recommendation on SAFE vs priced equity vs convertible note for the $200-250k round. Instrument drafted and ready to issue. Investor-friendly without giving away the house.
5
Investor Narrative Brief
One-page summary of the architecture, the structure, and the capital strategy. Written for the investor conversation, not for you. The questions they will ask about your setup are the ones this document is designed to answer before they ask them.
03 / What this does not include
Scope clarity prevents surprises.
  • Any advice touching a Rwanda partnership or third-party commercial arrangements. That is a separate engagement if and when you need it.
  • Dispute resolution or enforcement claims against Goliath. We are building forward, not backwards.
  • Local Ghanaian or Rwandan filing and registration. We coordinate with local counsel. Their fees are yours directly.
  • Incorporation fees and government filing costs. Billed to you at cost, no markup.
  • US tax advice. We flag the questions and point you to the right CPA. We are not licensed for US tax.
