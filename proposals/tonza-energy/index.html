Replacing charcoal with solar is a product insight. Scaling it is a company-building problem.

Prepared for Stephen Khan / Tonza Energy. July 2026. Valid 30 days.

The Situation

The problem you are solving is real. The window to capture it is not unlimited.

Stephen, charcoal kills slowly. It empties household budgets, fills lungs with smoke, and runs on a supply chain that deforests the continent. A solar cart that eliminates it at the point of use is a structural substitute for a billion-dollar informal market, and that is a genuinely compelling position to be building from.

You are now at the inflection point where product validation ends and company building begins. You need $50K for prototyping and $250K for deployment. You are pivoting to smart products, which means IP must be locked in before a single line of code is shared with a hardware partner or a new hire. And you need the legal foundation to approach impact investors and DFIs without a question mark over your structure.

Kenya's clean cooking market is moving fast. The government has committed to universal clean cooking access by 2030. Verified carbon credits from clean cooking are among the highest-generating in sub-Saharan Africa. The capital exists for the right operator. This engagement gets you ready to be that operator.

Workstream 1 deliverables

  • Entity review and structuring: Audit of existing Kenyan entity (or incorporation guidance if pending). Confirm the right structure for an impact fundraising round, including foreign investor compatibility and any holding company layer needed.
  • Cap table architecture: Founder allocation, vesting schedule, option pool sizing for future hires and advisors, pre-money structure ready for the first SAFE or priced seed round.
  • Founder and shareholder agreements: Founder rights, drag-along and tag-along, right of first refusal, anti-dilution framework, consent matters. Built for Kenya but legible to international impact investors.
  • IP assignment and smart product framework: All current and future IP (technology, firmware, sensor data, brand, designs) formally assigned from founders to the company. This is the first thing any serious investor checks on the smart product pivot. It must be done before hardware partner conversations begin.
  • SAFE template: Investor-ready Simple Agreement for Future Equity, calibrated to your raise size and valuation cap. Reduces legal cost on first close to near zero and keeps terms clean for subsequent rounds.
  • Governance framework: Board composition and decision rights for the seed stage, consent thresholds, officer roles, annual meeting requirements. Gives investors the assurance they need without giving up founder control prematurely.

Workstream 2 deliverables

  • Fundraising strategy memo: How much to raise, in what structure (SAFE vs grant vs DFI debt vs blended finance), from whom, and in what sequence. Clean cooking in Kenya has a specific capital map. We know it.
  • Impact investor and DFI targeting list: Curated shortlist of active funders: SunFunder, CrossBoundary Energy Access, Energy Catalyst (Innovate UK/FCDO), Africa Enterprise Challenge Fund, Clean Cooking Alliance Fund, SE4All Efficiency for Access, and relevant climate DFI lines active in Kenya.
  • Carbon credit pathway assessment: Clean cooking generates high-quality verified carbon credits under Gold Standard and Verra methodologies. If applicable to your product design, this changes your unit economics significantly. We assess the pathway and can connect you to a registered project developer.
  • Pitch materials review and narrative sharpening: Your pitch needs to lead with the Kenya market gap, the smart product differentiation, and the carbon angle. We refine the story so it lands with impact-first investors who see five decks a day.
  • Warm introductions: Pepe's East Africa network includes fund managers, DFI program officers, and operators who can make a warm intro worth ten cold emails. You approve every introduction before it goes out.
  • Term sheet review and negotiation advisory: When an investor comes with terms, we read it, tell you what is standard and what is not, and advise on what to push back on. Impact fund terms have specific patterns. We know them.
  • Closing coordination: SAFE or priced round documentation, investor onboarding agreements, closing checklist, executed documents filed and stored. Clean close, no loose ends.

Workstream 3 deliverables

  • Kenya operations lead search: Profile design, sourcing brief, and network introductions for a Kenya-based operations lead. The right person in Nairobi changes the deployment timeline by months. We help you define the role and open the network.
  • Co-founder governance protocol: Decision rights framework for the founding team. Who decides what, how disputes are resolved, what requires unanimous consent. Written before it is needed, so it is not drafted under pressure.
  • Hiring framework and first 3 hires: Role definitions, compensation benchmarks for Nairobi market, offer structure, and equity for early employees. Keeps the cap table clean as the team grows.
  • Strategic priority memos: Monthly one-page memos on the most important operational question Tonza is facing, written as a concrete recommendation with a specific decision attached.
  • Investor reporting setup: Template and cadence for post-close investor updates. Keeps your cap table warm, reduces noise, and positions you well for follow-on rounds.
  • Systems and processes: Mapping the operational workflows that need to exist before you scale, identifying the gaps, and building the lightweight internal systems that let a small team move fast without creating chaos. Efficiency before headcount.

Scope Boundaries

Scope clarity protects both sides.

  • We will not provide Kenyan domestic legal advice. Pepe Carrillo is not licensed to practice law in Kenya. This engagement provides deal structuring, governance design, and fundraising advisory. For local filings, regulatory submissions, and Kenya-specific compliance, we work alongside a Kenyan-licensed law firm. We make those introductions at no additional cost.
  • We will not engage directly with the Kenya Revenue Authority, Companies Registry, or other government bodies on your behalf without separate written authorization.
  • We will not make investor introductions without your prior approval of each target. You control who gets access to your company and your story.
  • Operations lead sourcing is advisory. We can advise on the profile, compensation structure, and offer terms for a Kenya operations lead, and make introductions from our East Africa network. Hiring decisions remain entirely with you.
