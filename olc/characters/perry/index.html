PERRY

The Good Investor

  • ArchetypeThe Good Investor
  • OriginNova Centauri
  • Alien level4/10 (Mantis)
  • One-lineWarm amber, iridescent cheekbone scales, full golden eyes, actually reads the deck

Appearance

Medium build with an open approachable posture. Warm amber/golden orange skin, the color of good honey in afternoon light. From a distance could pass as a very suntanned human. The two tells: iridescent scale-like patches along the cheekbones that shimmer faintly when light catches them, beautiful rather than unsettling, like a friendly dragon wearing a human suit and keeping only the best part. And the eyes: full golden irises with no visible white sclera, the whole eye warm gold, luminous and attentive. The genuine smile never stops, because it is actually genuine. Closest parallel: a friendly dragon who took humanoid form specifically to go into venture capital, kept only the cheekbone scales and the golden eyes, and is the only investor in the room who shows up to board meetings having read the material.

Outfit

Smart blazer over casual founder T-shirt underneath. Remembers where they came from.

Signature prop

A rolodex hologram of contacts, not cash. First move is always "let me introduce you to..."

Personality

Genuine value-add. Shows up to board meetings prepared. Adds connections before adding opinions. The endangered species.

Where they show up

"This is what good looks like" contrast stories, board seat stories where the investor earns their seat, pro-rata right stories from the founder's perspective.
