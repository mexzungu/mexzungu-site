ORI

The Technical Founder

  • ArchetypeThe Technical Founder
  • OriginOrion Nebula
  • Alien level4/10 (Mantis)
  • One-lineMale engineer, three floating code windows always orbiting his head

Appearance

Impossibly thin limbs, like someone stretched a human figure to 140% in the vertical axis only. Amber/burnt orange skin with a faintly translucent quality at the wrists and neck, where glowing blue circuit-pattern markings pulse underneath. Oversized eyes that dominate the face at about twice the expected size, dark and brilliant, the eyes from 1950s alien illustration. Wild physics-defying disheveled hair that points in 4 directions simultaneously, including against gravity. The hair is structural, not stylistic. Closest parallel: if E.T. grew up, enrolled at MIT on full scholarship, and never once thought to go home because the servers were interesting.

Outfit

Hoodie with obscure programming formula printed on front. Worn jeans. Plain nondescript sneakers.

Signature prop

Three floating holographic code windows orbiting him like satellites. Never closed.

Personality

Genius who is physically uncomfortable in any business conversation. Perpetual slight slouch. "Why are we talking about money when the algorithm isn't optimized."

Where they show up

Tech-heavy founding stories, IP assignment issues, technical co-founder dynamics, cap table stories where the CTO got diluted.
