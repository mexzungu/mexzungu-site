The DFI With Conditions

  • ArchetypeThe DFI With Conditions
  • OriginAuriga Constellation
  • Alien level5/10 (uncanny valley warmth)
  • One-lineHoney gold, calibrated warmth, uncanny valley precision, impact-metric mandate

Appearance

Lean, perfectly proportioned. Every measurement exactly where it should be, which is why something feels off. Honey gold skin (#FFC107) with a flawless matte quality. Hair that does not move. Fingers 10% longer than they should be. Irises a uniform warm amber, calibrated rather than natural. The face simulates warmth with precision. The smile is present and technically correct. Nothing is wrong. That is the problem. Reads as what an alien civilization would produce if asked to design "a credible institutional investor." Closest parallel: if the Vision (MCU) had been programmed with the mandate structure of a development finance institution, and had learned to express investment theses in the language of impact metrics.

Outfit

Perfectly pressed institutional blazer, no pocket square. Slim tailored trousers. Shoes that are expensive and appropriate. Statement watch that also tracks compliance milestones.

Signature prop

A floating side letter. Sixty pages. Subclauses visible, each with a compliance requirement attached. Behind it: the ESG impact framework. Behind that: the quarterly reporting dashboard. All glowing softly in gold.

Personality

Brings real credibility, real capital, and a real mandate. The capital is patient. The conditions are not. Everything in the term sheet is "standard for DFIs." The side letter is sixty pages of future obligations the founder will manage alone. Not malicious. Institutional. The conditions exist for good reasons. The founder needs a lawyer to read every single one before signing.

Where they show up

DFI term sheet stories, side letter education, compliance obligation stories, "what patient capital actually costs" beats, impact reporting obligation warnings, grant-to-equity conversion stories.
