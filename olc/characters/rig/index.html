RIG

The Technical Co-Founder (Exit Scenario)

  • ArchetypeThe Technical Co-Founder (Exit Scenario)
  • OriginRigel (blue supergiant)
  • Alien level3/10 (Drax)
  • One-lineDeep electric blue, luminous, purpose-built, terminator-adjacent

Appearance

Athletic compact build, nothing wasted. Deep electric blue skin with a faint luminosity, the color of a monitor in a dark room, or a lit screen seen through a wall. Zero decoration anywhere: cropped practical hair, strong jaw, expression of someone reading a codebase. Not cold, just focused. No ornamental features, nothing announces itself. The eyes are the only tell beyond the color: blue irises so saturated they verge on glowing, tracking everything, always actively processing. He reads as designed for a purpose. Built entirely for function. Closest parallel: if the Terminator had been rebuilt by alien engineers asked specifically to make something useful rather than threatening, it had decided to found a startup, and it had not told anyone what it was yet.

Outfit

Simple tech hoodie (no branding). Slim dark trousers. Plain low-profile sneakers. Nothing announces itself.

Signature prop

A data tablet displaying code repositories, commit history, and timestamped changelogs. The timestamp on the oldest entry predates the company. He is always reviewing something.

Personality

Technically brilliant and completely product-focused. Built the core IP before the company existed. Came to Earth to build something real. Did that. Then got a corporate offer three years in and realized he had been working in a governance vacuum the whole time. Not a villain. Not a bad partner. Just someone who deserved a better-drafted document and a lawyer who asked the right questions. "The code works. The agreement we signed doesn't."

Where they show up

Technical co-founder exits, IP assignment disputes, good/bad leaver provision stories, pre-incorporation IP ownership, situations where founding agreement gaps only become visible when someone wants out. Pairs naturally with CASSIE (Cassiopeia) in co-founder conflict storylines.
