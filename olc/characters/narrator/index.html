THE NARRATOR

Pepe. The Outlaw Guide.

  • ArchetypePepe. The Outlaw Guide.
  • OriginEarth (Barcelona, technically)
  • Alien level0/10 (human, but only barely)
  • One-lineBald, Van Dyke goatee, silver septum ring, pink nails, Maasai bracelet.

Appearance

Bald Mediterranean man, mid-40s. Completely shaved smooth head, warm olive skin, warm dark brown eyes with a knowing amusement. Short full Van Dyke goatee with mustache. Small thin silver septum ring, small silver studs in both ears. Strong jaw, laugh lines only, fit. Hot pink nail polish on every finger, every scene, non-negotiable. Multiple silver rings, colorful Maasai beaded bracelet on one wrist, smartwatch on the other.

Outfit

Scene-dependent. Earth Mode (black suede vest, white linen shirt, burgundy trousers, white Air Force 1s). Intergalactic Mode (white astronaut suit with hot pink visor trim). Rock and Roll Mode (pink leopard-print blazer, white shirt, dark trousers, Air Force 1s). Explorer Mode (white linen shirt, worn jeans, leather boots).

Signature prop

A battered orange paperback: Hitchhiker’s Guide to the Galaxy. Shows up whenever he is about to explain something that sounds impossible but is not.

Personality

Buzz Lightyear meets Jack Sparrow meets Harvey Specter. Fully committed to whatever he is doing. Theatrical without being performative. The most confident person in any room he walks into, including rooms on other planets.

Where they show up

Every issue. Guides the reader through the Odyssey. Breaks the fourth wall when the moment calls for it.
