Founder

AURA

The Mission-Driven Founder

  • ArchetypeThe Mission-Driven Founder
  • OriginAurora Borealis
  • Alien level4/10 (Mantis)
  • One-linePurpose-first, values-driven, builds companies that mean something

Appearance

Medium build humanoid alien woman. Coral/warm magenta skin in standby state, soft and lovely. When talking about the mission (which is most of the time), the bioluminescence intensifies from background warmth to a visible glow, like a phone screen in a dark room, bright enough to illuminate faces of people she's talking to. Flowing hair with tips that glow faintly at all times, trailing behind her like bioluminescent sea creatures. Large earnest eyes with a slight upward tilt. Always slightly forward-leaning in posture, always mid-lean toward you, always about to say something important. Closest parallel: if Mantis (Guardians of the Galaxy) had channeled her empathy powers into founding a social enterprise instead of managing a narcissistic celestial being.

Outfit

Ethically sourced jacket. Organic everything. Tiny solar panel accessories on shoulders.

Signature prop

Impact metrics hologram always floating open: Lives Changed: 4,721 / Revenue: TBD.

Personality

Genuinely changing the world. Slightly annoying about it. Will find a way to make every conversation about the mission.

Where they show up

Impact/social enterprise legal structures, PBC stories, grant vs. equity stories, mission drift after fundraising.
