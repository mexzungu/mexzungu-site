CASSIE

The Bootstrapper

  • ArchetypeThe Bootstrapper
  • OriginCassiopeia
  • Alien level2/10 (Gamora)
  • One-lineFemale founder, allergic to dilution, precise minimal aesthetic

Appearance

The most human-looking character on the crew besides Pepe and Kellen. From 50 feet, she is just a person. The only tell is the forest green skin, dark and matte like deep moss on stone. No antennae, no markings, no alien tech, no bioluminescence. Nothing announces itself. Skin has a weathered texture, the texture of someone who has spent more time on worksites than in WeWork spaces. Strong jaw. The perpetually narrowed eyes are not aggressive; they are the eyes of someone who has read 200 term sheets and spotted the same predatory clause in 40 different fonts. Closest parallel: a human accountant from Glasgow, rendered in dark forest green, with the trust setting permanently at 3/10.

Outfit

Simple work shirt. Practical trousers. Sturdy boots. Zero startup aesthetic. Zero tech aesthetic.

Signature prop

Actual paper notebook. Actual pen behind ear. Zero holographic devices. Deliberate.

Personality

Bootstrapped to €2M ARR and physically allergic to dilution. Arms always crossed. Trusts nobody with a term sheet.

Where they show up

Bootstrap vs. VC comparison stories, dilution warnings, option pool stories, "what you give up when you raise" narratives.
