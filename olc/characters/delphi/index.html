Investor

DELPHI

The Patient Builder

  • ArchetypeThe Patient Builder
  • OriginDelphinus
  • Alien level3/10 (Drax)
  • One-lineDeep wine red, klingon-adjacent forehead ridges, unhurried, compound interest man

Appearance

Solid unhurried build, the kind of presence that makes a room feel calmer. Deep wine-red skin, the color of good Rioja held up to light, warm and matte. The forehead ridges are the only obvious alien feature: subtle horizontal bony lines running above each eyebrow, parallel and spaced evenly, like a Klingon in a more forgiving universe. Just pronounced enough that you cannot ignore them but not aggressive. Strong square jaw set in perpetual composure. Calm steady eyes with dark irises that have seen market cycles. Expression of someone who has never once refreshed their TechCrunch funding announcement, because there is not one. Closest parallel: a Klingon (Star Trek) who abandoned the honor code at 22, discovered compound interest, and has been growing quietly at 15% MoM for seven years while everyone else was on CNBC.

Outfit

Clean simple timeless clothing. Nothing trendy, nothing dated.

Signature prop

Leather-bound paper notebook. Old pen. A finished coffee (not for show). Zero holographic tech visible anywhere.

Personality

Profitable since month 8. Growing 15% MoM for 7 years. No VC. No press. The slightly smug silence.

Where they show up

Revenue-first structure stories, "you don't need to raise" contrasts, shareholder agreement stories where the bootstrapper finally takes one investor.
