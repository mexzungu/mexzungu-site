Founder

SIRIX

The Serial Entrepreneur

  • ArchetypeThe Serial Entrepreneur
  • OriginSirius
  • Alien level5/10 (Nebula)
  • One-lineMulti-company builder, works in parallel, seen it before

Appearance

Medium lean build. Metallic silver matte skin, the quality of brushed steel, not shiny, not reflective, like it has absorbed too much light over too many years and stopped giving it back. Faint cybernetic temple implants that are small and slightly misaligned, clearly retrofitted after the fact rather than original equipment ("from that rough Series A"). Face is a map of earned mistakes: faint scar along the jaw from a deal that went wrong, small chip in one ear from a board meeting that went sideways. Sardonic half-smile that never quite returns to neutral. Closest parallel: if The Silver Surfer (Marvel) resigned as Galactus's herald, built two companies, sold one, buried one, and is currently mid-way through attempt three.

Outfit

Worn leather jacket with embroidered company logo patches on the back. Each patch is a previous company. Some burned or faded.

Signature prop

Wrist hologram counter: Companies 3 / Exits 1 / Failures 2 / Currently: attempt 3.

Personality

"I know exactly where this is going." Seen every mistake. Occasionally wrong about new kinds of mistakes. The voice of experience. Sometimes right, sometimes dangerously overconfident.

Where they show up

Repeat founder stories, "learn from the last company" beats, vesting cliff stories, founder agreement disputes.
