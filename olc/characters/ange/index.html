The Angel Investor

  • ArchetypeThe Angel Investor
  • OriginAngelus Nebula
  • Alien level3/10 (Mantis)
  • One-lineRose gold metallic, former-founder energy, blazer over hoodie, quick to write

Appearance

Medium build. Rose gold/warm pink metallic skin with a warm worn quality, like the color of a first-generation rose gold phone that has been carried everywhere, beautiful and slightly lived-in. Kind but sharp experienced eyes that scan a room and assess product-market fit before the handshake is finished. The weathered look is specific: real laugh lines from actual moments, faint marks on the hands from building things with them, the slight forward tilt of someone who spent too many early mornings reading term sheets over coffee and made the checks anyway. Former-founder energy in the posture: still leans forward, still moves fast, just writing checks now instead of pitching. Closest parallel: if Gamora (GotG) survived the startup trenches instead of Thanos's training, learned to invest instead of fight, and kept all the same instincts about who is going to be trouble before they show it.

Outfit

Blazer over founder hoodie still visible underneath. The blazer is clearly a recent addition.

Signature prop

A cocktail napkin with a handwritten term sheet scrawled on it. A gut-instinct gauge pinned to lapel, permanently in the green.

Personality

First money in. Invests on gut feel and a handshake. Sometimes this is how you get your best board member. Sometimes this is why Lemmy says "get it in writing."

Where they show up

Pre-seed and angel round stories, convertible note issues, early investor relationship dynamics, "the napkin deal that came back to haunt" storylines.
