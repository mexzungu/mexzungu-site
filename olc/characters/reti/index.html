RETI

The Aggressive VC

  • ArchetypeThe Aggressive VC
  • OriginZeta Reticuli
  • Alien level7/10 (Thanos-adjacent)
  • One-lineCrimson lacquered, two-too-many-teeth smile, thanos-adjacent, red-flag briefcase

Appearance

6'4", angular everywhere, crimson skin with an almost lacquered quality, like expensive furniture. Cheekbones technically too high. Jaw technically too defined. The whole face is gorgeous in a precisely-calibrated-but-slightly-wrong way, like a human face rendered by an AI trained exclusively on VC headshots. The smile is the tell: there are two too many teeth, and all of them are the exact same shape and size, which is not how teeth work. Always smiling. Eyes are dark with zero readable warmth, visibly busy with calculations behind them. Closest parallel: if Thanos (MCU) traded the Infinity Gauntlet for a carried interest agreement, kept the calculating eyes and the sense of inevitability, and told every founder he was "very aligned with your vision."

Outfit

Immaculate dark suit with subtle red pattern. Expensive watch. Everything tailored, nothing approachable.

Signature prop

Briefcase that opens to reveal stacks of term sheets with literal tiny red flags sticking out.

Personality

"We're very founder-friendly." (They are not.) Charming until the liquidation preference clause comes up.

Where they show up

Liquidation preference horror stories, pro-rata clause traps, board control loss stories, drag-along clause reveals, any story where the investor is the problem.
