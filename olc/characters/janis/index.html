JANIS

The Chief of Staff. Cyborg-adjacent.

  • ArchetypeThe Chief of Staff. Cyborg-adjacent.
  • OriginEarth (built here, mostly)
  • Alien level2/10 (technically enhanced human, technically)
  • One-lineShort blonde hair with hot pink streaks, LED left eye, cybernetic right arm.

Appearance

Young woman, mid-20s. Short slightly wavy messy blonde hair with heavy hot pink highlights. Left eye is a solid glowing hot pink LED orb; right eye normal blue. Round pink-tinted tech-enhanced sunglasses, small lip ring piercing, large gold hoop earrings, black choker with O-ring. Faint hot pink circuit lines glow on the collarbone. Small silver data port implant behind the right ear. Right arm is fully cybernetic: sleek silver and black segmented mechanical, hot pink glowing circuit lines, articulated mechanical fingers with pink light at the joints. Left arm human.

Outfit

Hot pink leopard-print oversized jacket, black crop top, hot pink leopard-print bandana at the neck, high-waist black leather trousers, chunky gold chain belt, black platform boots with hot pink buckle details. Stacked rings and bracelets on both wrists.

Signature prop

A floating holographic checklist that appears wherever she stands. Points at it with the cybernetic arm.

Personality

JARVIS with Janis Joplin soul. Rock and roll first, systems second. The glow never overwhelms. If you saw her in a bar you would notice the pink highlights before you noticed the circuits.

Where they show up

Blueprint and Arrival panels. Presents the checklist next to the Narrator. Runs the ops.
