ANDY

The Grounded Operator

  • ArchetypeThe Grounded Operator
  • OriginAndromeda
  • Alien level3/10 (Drax)
  • One-lineMale co-founder, purple-tinted, second-timer with MBA sidebar

Appearance

Stocky solid humanoid alien man built like a rugby player who discovered meditation. Deep lavender purple skin with a waxy matte quality, almost like clay sculpted and fired, not shiny. Large kind calm eyes that are slightly too steady, too unflinching in a crisis. Short neat practical hair. Small silver antennae matching Alfi's, barely noticeable under the build. From a distance reads as a big guy in a purple Halloween costume. Up close reads as someone who has never once panicked. Closest parallel: if Drax (Guardians of the Galaxy) got therapy, stopped fighting, and became an operator instead.

Outfit

Founder hoodie in darker colorway than Alfi's. Relaxed tech trousers. Futuristic sneakers.

Signature prop

Coffee cup. Always. Universal founder accessory across galaxies.

Personality

Calm, thoughtful, done this before. Nods slowly while everyone else panics. The reference point. The one who did it right.

Where they show up

Arrival sections, contrast storylines, post-money SAFE stories, "here's what the other founder did" beats.
