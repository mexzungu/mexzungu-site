Founder

LYRI

The Pitch Queen

  • ArchetypeThe Pitch Queen
  • OriginLyra Constellation
  • Alien level3/10 (Nebula lite)
  • One-lineEmerald green female founder, sharp confident, moves fast signs fast

Appearance

6'2" and moves like the floor is a runway that extends to the horizon. Emerald green skin in neutral state, just green and striking. When presenting, the bioluminescent patterns activate: flowing emerald lines light up along the cheekbones and collarbone, like circuitry brought to life. Sharp angular cheekbones you could use as a straight edge. Sleek dramatic hair that falls perfectly, apparently defying normal hair physics through force of personality. Perfect commanding posture, chin always at exactly the right angle. Closest parallel: if Gamora (MCU) had trained with TED speakers instead of assassins, and channeled all that intensity into closing investor rooms.

Outfit

Sharp power blazer over sleek top. Tailored trousers. Statement boots. Always looks like she walked off a TED stage.

Signature prop

Holographic pitch deck slides materialize around her like playing cards. She flicks them at people.

Personality

Can close any room in three minutes. Magnetic. Less clear on what happens after the raise closes.

Where they show up

Fundraising stories, term sheet negotiations, investor deck issues, "raised the money but not on the right terms" storylines.
