KELLEN

The Operator. Human.

  • ArchetypeThe Operator. Human.
  • OriginEarth (Kenya)
  • Alien level0/10 (real human, grounded operator)
  • One-lineBlack woman, late 20s, short shoulder-length locs, rose-gold rectangular glasses, wide bright smile.

Appearance

Black woman, late 20s. Short shoulder-length locs. Rose-gold rectangular glasses. Very bright wide smile. Warm dark skin. The energy of someone perpetually holding multiple things at once (coffee, clipboard, a document she has not read yet). Slightly overwhelmed. Fully competent.

Outfit

Working clothes. Grounded. Nothing announces itself.

Signature prop

A clipboard and a coffee, both usually half-empty.

Personality

Janis energy with less chaos and more execution. The one who actually gets the thing done while the aliens argue.

Where they show up

Ground Control scenes. Real-world followups. When the story needs a human operator behind the curtain.
