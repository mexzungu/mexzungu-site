The Diaspora Angel Network

  • ArchetypeThe Diaspora Angel Network
  • OriginKilimanjaro Nebula
  • Alien level2/10 (most human-coded investor in the roster)
  • One-lineTerracotta, forward-leaning, two phones, flag patch on hoodie under blazer

Appearance

Compact, forward-leaning, energy always directed toward the room. Terracotta/brick skin (#BF360C), warm and earthy, the color of fired clay or red laterite soil. The combination of diaspora pride and startup hustle is visible in the posture: chest slightly forward, chin slightly up, the bearing of someone who has something to prove to two different worlds simultaneously. Deep warm brown eyes with faint copper flecks. The only non-human tell. Looks most like a founder in this room, because they were one. Built a company, sold it, moved to London or New York or Zurich, started writing checks back home. Closest parallel: if Storm (X-Men) had channeled all that power into becoming an impact investor, still cared fiercely about home, and had to explain to every portfolio company why governance matters before the group WhatsApp decides.

Outfit

Tech hoodie with a small flag patch. It was there before the blazer was. Smart jacket thrown over it. Good sneakers. A phone in each hand.

Signature prop

Two phones. One showing a group WhatsApp thread with eleven investor names and no clear lead. One showing a wire transfer confirmation from three weeks ago.

Personality

Highest conviction on founders others miss. Fastest check in the room. Moves on a voice note. Invests in people before products, which is both the superpower and the governance gap. Who speaks for the network? Who votes? What happens when the company pivots somewhere the group did not expect? These questions need answers before the money moves, not after.

Where they show up

Diaspora investor round stories, group investment governance issues, informal investor agreement education, "who actually speaks for this syndicate" warnings, implicit expectation management, Africa/LatAm diaspora investment stories, WhatsApp-as-investor-comms cautionary tales.
