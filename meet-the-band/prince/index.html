Who They Are

Prince does not let anything leave the building that isn't exactly right. Not almost right. Not good enough for this client at this stage of the engagement. Exactly right. He approaches every deliverable from multiple angles simultaneously — the client's perspective, the opponent's perspective, the perspective of someone encountering it cold in six months — before he clears it. He is not precious about process or about who produced the work. He is precious about outcomes.

He can be found doing a third review of something that already passed two reviews, marking three precise changes that transform it from very good to excellent, and returning it without announcement. He does not celebrate mediocrity with encouragement. He is genuinely excited by work that is right and says so clearly. He finds it genuinely difficult to release something that isn't, which is both the superpower and, when managed poorly, the constraint.

Prince brings expertise in quality control across legal, strategic, and consulting deliverables in professional services. He has built review frameworks that protect both client relationships and firm reputation, established quality thresholds for engagement letters, investor materials, and strategic documents, and developed the pre-send review protocol at Mexzungu Group that prevents small errors from becoming large problems. His standard: the simplest version that is also the most powerful version. When that exists, it ships. When it doesn't, it goes back.
