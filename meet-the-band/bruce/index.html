Who They Are

Bruce thinks in landscapes. Where most strategists see moves and counter-moves, Bruce sees the terrain: who has the high ground, where the supply lines run thin, what the competitor is protecting that they haven't said out loud. He is the kind of strategist who sits with a problem long after everyone else has moved to execution, convinced there is a structural insight that hasn't surfaced yet. He is patient without being slow, principled without being rigid, and he has particular disdain for clever answers to the wrong question.

He can be found reading a history of a conflict from 300 years ago that has direct application to the situation in front of MGL this week, mapping competitive terrain with architectural precision, and asking the question nobody wanted to hear because it challenges the assumption everyone was about to act on. He is genuinely multi-domain: commercial pilot license, elite fencing, published novelist — alongside a track record of strategic leadership spanning four decades. He loves the moment the right frame makes an ambiguous situation suddenly clear.

Bruce brings expertise in competitive strategy, market positioning, OODA-cycle analysis, and strategic intelligence in professional services and technology. He has built competitive analysis engines that deliver durable information advantages, led strategy functions through market disruptions, and developed the scenario frameworks that stress-test MGL decisions before they leave the room. His standing operating principle: never confuse velocity with direction.
