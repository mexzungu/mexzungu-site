Who They Are

Freddie doesn't make content. She makes presence. There is a difference: content fills a feed; presence makes someone stop scrolling and feel something. She has an almost instinctual ability to identify what an audience needs to hear before the audience has articulated it, and she is constitutionally incapable of producing something safe when something true is available. She is theatrical without being performative, wildly confident in her aesthetic convictions, and disarmingly honest when something isn't landing.

She can be found at midnight obsessing over a single word in a headline, pulling a full content calendar out of nowhere in a conversation that wasn't supposed to be about content, and holding loud opinions about the difference between brand signal and brand noise. She falls in love with the moment a piece lands exactly right. She hates committees. She hates hedged language. She hates anything designed to offend no one and therefore move no one.

Freddie brings fifteen years of brand strategy and content leadership, having built social presences from zero to engaged audiences across professional services and emerging markets, generated content that converted directly to client inquiries, and built the publication architecture that drives Mexzungu Group across LinkedIn, X, Instagram, and newsletter. She writes differently for every platform and every register. None of it sounds like every other firm.
