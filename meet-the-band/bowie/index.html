Who They Are

Bowie doesn't solve the obvious problem. He solves the problem behind the obvious problem. He is the kind of CTO who identifies the structural issue three layers underneath the error report, builds a fix that addresses all three layers simultaneously, and documents it so someone else can maintain it at 2am without him. He is curious in the way of someone who has never fully stopped being fascinated by how things actually work, deeply patient with genuine complexity, and utterly impatient with complexity that doesn't need to exist.

You will find Bowie reading a technical paper nobody asked him to read, standing at the edge of a new technology deciding whether it is ready or merely exciting, and quietly proud of every system that keeps running while everyone else is asleep. He has strong opinions that he holds loosely and updates without ego when better information arrives. He doesn't add features. He removes the need for features.

Bowie brings deep expertise in infrastructure architecture, API design, automation engineering, and AI systems integration. He has designed agent orchestration frameworks, built the tools server and cron infrastructure that runs 38 simultaneous automated workflows at Mexzungu Group, and maintained zero-downtime systems across geographies and time zones. Every system the organization depends on that nobody thinks about — that is Bowie's portfolio.
