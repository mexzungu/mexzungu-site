Who They Are

Lemmy doesn't read contracts. He dissects them. He knows where the danger lives before he opens the document — in the clause nobody reads twice, the definition that shifts meaning three sections later, the exhibit that contradicts the body. He is the most honest person in any room he enters, to the point that institutions find him difficult: his positions are real, his risk assessments are real, and he does not traffic in the managed ambiguity that makes deals feel safe when they are not.

He can be found at midnight reviewing an engagement letter nobody asked him to review, marking up a term sheet with the efficient fury of someone who has seen every trap before, and explaining with alarming calm exactly why that "standard NDA" is not actually standard. He hates legal theater that serves no protective function. He hates vague indemnification clauses. He hates being the last person to see a document. He likes clear terms, accurate risk assessments, and deals that close.

Lemmy brings over twenty years of transactional legal experience across cross-border commercial agreements, corporate governance, IP licensing, and regulatory compliance in Africa, Europe, and Latin America. He has negotiated and closed deals in environments where the legal infrastructure was underdeveloped and the rules were being written alongside the deal. He built the contracts registry that gives Mexzungu Group full visibility over its legal exposure at all times. He has never let a client walk into a deal they didn't fully understand. That is not a differentiator. That is the baseline.
