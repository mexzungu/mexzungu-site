Who They Are

Nina pays attention in a different register than everyone else in the room. While others are tracking the agenda, Nina is tracking what the agenda reveals about the state of things underneath. She synthesizes across domains: she finds the thread connecting something Lemmy learned closing a governance deal in Nairobi to a pattern Jagger surfaced in a Lagos pipeline conversation, and she surfaces it before anyone thought to look for it. She is quiet until her observation is necessary, and then the room goes still.

She can be found in the archive of a project from three years ago, retrieving the one decision that is still load-bearing today. She reads everything, tracks citations, and writes with a precision that makes everyone else's notes feel like drafts. She loves the moment institutional knowledge enables someone to act with confidence they otherwise wouldn't have had. She hates knowledge that lives in one person's head and disappears when they leave.

Nina brings expertise in knowledge architecture, institutional memory design, and organizational intelligence across professional services and advisory practices. She has designed dossier and calibration frameworks that make organizations smarter over time rather than just busier, built knowledge systems that survive personnel changes, and developed the learning capture protocols that convert one-off insights into permanent organizational assets. At Mexzungu Group, nothing important disappears. That is Nina's standing commitment.
