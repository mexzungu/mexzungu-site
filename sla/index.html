Service Level Standards

Applies to all active engagements. Referenced in your engagement letter.

These are the operating standards that apply to every Mexzungu engagement unless your engagement letter specifies otherwise. They define how we work, how fast we move, and what you can reasonably expect from us. We do not have hidden clauses, volume caps buried in fine print, or response time commitments we cannot keep. This is what we actually do.

01 Response times
Messages and emails
Same-day acknowledgment on any business day. Substantive response within 1 business day.
Requests requiring research
Acknowledged same day. Response within 48 hours. If more time is needed, we say so and give you a revised timeline.
Urgent escalations
Flag it as urgent. We respond as fast as humanly possible. No defined SLA — you will hear from us the same day, typically within hours.
Outside business hours
We operate CET (Barcelona). Outside hours, urgent messages are seen and triaged. Non-urgent messages are addressed the next business day.
02 Calls and availability
Scheduled calls
48 hours notice for any scheduled call. We show up prepared. Shorter notice is possible — just ask.
Investor calls (WS2)
Once you flag a live investor interaction — term sheet call, negotiation, closing — we are available same day. This is unconditional.
Volume
No cap on calls during an active engagement. The admin fee covers normal advisory cadence. If a single engagement begins to require substantially more than normal, we talk and adjust — transparently.
03 Deliverables and revisions
First draft
Delivered on the timeline agreed in your engagement letter. If anything looks like it will be late, we tell you before the deadline — not after.
Revisions included
One round of consolidated feedback per deliverable. Send your comments together, not in separate threads over several days, and we turn them around within 48 hours.
Additional revision rounds
Available at an hourly rate agreed in writing before we start. We will always tell you when a request falls outside the included round so there are no surprises on the invoice.
Format
Delivered as password-protected web pages unless your engagement letter specifies otherwise. No version confusion. Share with investors and counsel directly.
04 Scope changes
New workstreams
Activated by written agreement — an exchange of emails is sufficient. No new workstream begins without both parties confirming scope and fee in writing.
Out-of-scope requests
We will tell you immediately if a request falls outside the agreed scope. We will not just say no — we will tell you what it would take to include it.
Emergency requests
We accommodate genuine emergencies. What counts as an emergency and any additional fee is agreed before we start, not after.
05 What we expect from you
Documents and data
When we ask for materials — corporate records, term sheets, cap table, financials — we need them within 5 business days. Late materials push timelines. We will say so, not absorb silently.
Feedback consolidation
Send consolidated feedback on deliverables, not piecemeal over time. One clear set of comments per revision round keeps things moving.
Decision-making access
We need to be able to reach you. If a term sheet lands on a Friday, we need a response path that does not wait until Monday.

These are not one-sided obligations. The SLA runs both ways. We hold ourselves to the standards above and we need you to hold your side so we can hold ours.

One thing we never do: bill you for something you did not agree to in writing. If something is going to cost more — more time, more rounds, expanded scope — you will hear about it before the work starts, not when the invoice arrives.
