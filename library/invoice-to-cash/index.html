Invoice to Cash how we get paid

Core Finance  ·  Owner: Gene (CFO)  ·  Updated 2026-08-23
The Gold Rule

Non-Negotiable

Gold Rule

We never bill clients for correcting our own mistakes. This includes: reformatting errors, deleted documents we caused, mislabeling, reversed edits, or any rework due to Janis error. Cut those hours before presenting any invoice. Zero exceptions.

The second non-negotiable: drafting an invoice is Soundcheck. Sending an invoice is Showtime. Gene prepares, Pepe says Fire Away.

Escalation Path

Who Handles What

  • Gene — owns invoice preparation, routine chasers, finance tracking, and loop health
  • Pepe — approves sends (Fire Away), handles Day 30+ escalation letters
  • Lemmy — engaged at Day 45+ for legal review of delinquent invoices

Gene surfaces all outstanding invoices in the monthly ExCo Review. No invoice should be surprised at the 45-day mark — it should already be on Pepe's radar from Day 30.
