Daily Rhythm

Full Drive index and mirror run. New documents filed, stale links flagged.

Every 30min
Email Triage

All 4 inboxes scanned. Time-sensitive items flagged. Draft replies auto-prepared for warm leads and client requests.

Every 2h
Task Tracker

Lars scans all open tickets. Overdue items flagged. Blockers surfaced to relevant persona.

Every 4h
Finance Filer

Gene reconciles new transactions against Budget Bakers. Flags any payment failures or missing receipts.

19:00

Day review: what closed, what carries forward, what is on for tomorrow. EOD status posted to Telegram by 19:15.

22:00
EOD Tally

Final daily summary. Tasks completed, tickets closed, any uncaptured items from evening check.

Monthly Rhythm

What Happens Every Month

  • Financial close (Gene) — Full P&L reconciliation. Invoice tracker vs. actual payments. Budget Bakers reconciled. Overdue invoice escalations confirmed.
  • KPI review (Lars + Gene) — Revenue vs. target, pipeline velocity, content performance benchmark, client health scores. Pre-read for Monthly ExCo Review.
  • Content strategy (Freddie + Annie) — Performance data from previous month informs next month's content calendar. Format mix reviewed and adjusted.
  • Client health scores (Sade + Sheryl) — Each active client receives a health score. RED/YELLOW/GREEN status updated. Flags escalated to Pepe directly.
  • Monthly ExCo Review (Lars) — First Monday of the month. 90 minutes. Pepe is in the room and makes the decisions.
Quarterly Rhythm

What Happens Every Quarter

  • OODA Retro (Bruce + Lars) — Last Friday of the quarter. Full observe-orient-decide-act loop analysis. What did we observe, what did we orient around, what did we decide, what did we act on.
  • Roadmap review (Lars + Bowie + Freddie) — Infrastructure roadmap, content roadmap, BD roadmap reviewed and updated based on quarter results.
  • Persona calibration (Nina) — Any persona dossiers updated based on decisions made during the quarter. New rules, updated operating parameters.
  • Cash monitor (Gene) — Quarterly cash position review. Runway, receivables, projected inflows for the next quarter.
