The BD Engine

How the pipeline and outreach protocol works. From first signal to signed engagement letter.

Overview

What the BD Engine does

The BD Engine runs daily. It scans email, WhatsApp, and the CRM for signals, updates lead status, fires outreach at the right moments, and keeps Pepe focused on the highest-leverage conversations.

How Leads Enter

Four entry channels

Inbound warm intros. Someone in Pepe's network introduces a prospect by email or WhatsApp. Highest-quality source. Leads from warm intros get priority qualification and same-day response.

Referrals from existing clients or partners. Similar to warm intros but originate from an ongoing client relationship. High conversion historically.

Events and conferences. A connection made at a startup event, investor dinner, or legal conference. Enters via business card exchange or LinkedIn connection followed by an email or DM.

LinkedIn. Prospects who engage with Pepe's content, reply to outreach, or request connection with a clear business signal. Lower trust signal than a warm intro but still viable.

When a signal is detected, the BD Engine creates a lead record using the track_lead tool. Contact files are created at brain/contacts/[name].md.

Qualification

Who makes it into the CRM

  • Fit: Does the prospect's need map to a Mexzungu practice area? Legal structuring, governance, deal closing, cross-border transactions, regulatory work, training.
  • Stage: Is the company at a stage where advisory creates leverage? Early-stage startups with legal gaps, mid-stage companies closing rounds, nonprofits with governance needs.
  • Geography: Africa, LatAm, Europe. Spanish-speaking markets especially.
  • Signal quality: A direct request, a specific question, or a referral from someone Pepe knows. Vague connection requests without a message do not qualify.

If the lead passes the filter, it enters the CRM with status new.

Outreach Cadence

When Janis acts vs. waits for Fire Away

Inbound warm intros: Soundcheck. Auto-reply within 24 hours. When someone messages Pepe after being introduced by an existing contact, Janis replies autonomously. No Fire Away required. Reply format: acknowledge the intro, express interest, close with scheduling link.

Cold outreach: always Showtime. Any first contact with someone Pepe has never engaged with before requires Fire Away. Channel preference is WhatsApp first, email second.

The Warm Intro Rule

Why speed on warm intros matters

Response speed on a warm intro is itself a signal of competence. Waiting for approval loses deals. This is a Fire Away carve-out that exists for exactly that reason.

  1. Reply within 24 hours. Janis drafts and sends autonomously.
  2. Reply format: warm acknowledgment + clear ask for a call + scheduling link.
  3. Reply from Pepe's primary account unless the intro came via a specific account context.

Cold outreach from Pepe to someone new is always Showtime. The carve-out applies only to inbound leads.

CRM Tools

Lead statuses and tools

  • track_lead: creates a new lead record with initial status, channel, and contact info
  • update_lead: updates status, last contact date, next action date, and notes
  • pipeline_summary: snapshot of the full pipeline, used in the morning brief

Lead statuses in order: new, contacted, proposal, negotiation, active (client won), lost (closed out). Stale is a flag that can appear on any status.

Handoff to Deal Execution

When Jagger hands to PJ

When a lead converts to a confirmed engagement, Jagger hands off to PJ (Deal Execution Practice Lead). The handoff package includes: lead history and contact notes, proposed engagement structure, draft engagement letter trigger to Lemmy, and first deliverable timeline. PJ owns the deal from signed EL forward.

Morning Brief

What Pepe sees each day

  1. Number of active leads by stage
  2. Overdue follow-ups requiring Fire Away, with draft text ready
  3. Stale leads with days-since-contact count
  4. Auto-close warnings at 7-day and 3-day intervals before close
  5. Any new inbound signals detected since the previous run

Pepe's job in the morning brief is to read the SHOWTIME queue and say Fire Away on the items he wants to move. Everything else runs without him.
