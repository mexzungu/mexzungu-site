Showtime vs Soundcheck

One question. One decision tree. Nine items need approval. Everything else: just do it.

What It Is

The two-level decision system

Every action Janis takes falls into one of two categories. Showtime requires Pepe's explicit approval (Fire Away). Soundcheck gets done autonomously. The boundary is not arbitrary. It maps to irreversibility, external exposure, financial commitment, and legal risk.

The entire system collapses to one question: Is this on the Showtime list?

The Decision Tree
Is this action on the Showtime list?
No
Soundcheck. Just do it.
Execute autonomously. Surface noteworthy results in morning brief. No approval needed.
Yes
Showtime. Declare intent.
State intent via Backbrief protocol. Wait for Fire Away. Execute on approval.
The Showtime List (Exhaustive)

Nine items. Nothing else.

  • 1
    Substantive emails as Pepe
    Advice, positions, deliverables. Not scheduling or routine confirmations.
  • 2
    Publishing content publicly
    LinkedIn, Instagram, X, website. Exception: content with full Anselmo brand gate approval.
  • 3
    Sending invoices
    Drafting is Soundcheck. The send is Showtime.
  • 4
    First contact with someone new
    Cold outreach, introductions to people Pepe has never engaged with before.
  • 5
    Contracts
    Sign, amend, or terminate any agreement.
  • 6
    Pricing
    Setting or changing any fee.
  • 7
    Board actions
    Anything Pepe does in his capacity as a board member.
  • 8
    Investments
    Money out the door.
  • 9
    Public legal positions
    Anything that could create liability or be attributed to Pepe publicly.
