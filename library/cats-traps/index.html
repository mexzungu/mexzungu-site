CATs and TRAPs

The intellectual backbone of every client engagement. Build CATs and TRAPs to control RATs. This is the whole consulting philosophy.

What It Is

A system for managing legal risk through leverage

CATs and TRAPs is the strategic framework Pepe developed for structuring consulting engagements. It maps every client deliverable to one of two categories: things that advise and transact (CATs), and things that prevent and protect (TRAPs). Together they address the underlying threat: the RAT.

It is not a checklist. It is a lens for diagnosing what a client actually needs and structuring the engagement accordingly.

The Core Equation
CATs + TRAPs = control RATs

The Breakdown
CATs
The instruments of doing deals
  • C
    Contracts
    Shareholder agreements, employment contracts, engagement letters, SHAs, NDAs, term sheets, MOUs. The legal architecture of every relationship.
  • A
    Advice
    Strategic legal counsel, deal structuring, jurisdiction analysis, risk assessment, negotiation support. The thinking, not just the docs.
  • T
    Transactions
    Capital raises, acquisitions, cross-border structuring, investment instruments (SAFE, convertible notes), mergers, fund flows.
TRAPs
The instruments of protection
  • T
    Training
    Legal literacy programs, governance workshops, board education, team legal upskilling. Prevention through knowledge.
  • R
    Regulatory
    GDPR, data protection, sector-specific compliance, licensing, regulatory monitoring, filings. Staying inside the lines.
  • A
    Assessments
    Legal health checks, due diligence, cap table audits, governance reviews, risk mapping. Knowing where you stand.
  • P
    Policies
    Internal policies, codes of conduct, data handling procedures, board charters, dispute resolution frameworks. The operating rules.
What We Are Managing
RAT
Risk at Tolerance. Every client has a threshold for legal, regulatory, and operational risk. The goal is not to eliminate risk. That is impossible. The goal is to bring risk within the client's defined tolerance using the right mix of CATs and TRAPs. When risk exceeds tolerance, something breaks. CATs and TRAPs are the mechanism to keep it inside the line.
