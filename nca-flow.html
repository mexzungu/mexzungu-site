NCA Huesca: Transaction Flow

Path C — Arrendamiento con Opción de Compra → GO/NO-GO Vote → Contrato de Compraventa

Step 1 · Now
Sign Option Agreement
⚖ Legal
Arrendamiento con Opción de Compra signed and registered in the Registro de la Propiedad. Purchase price locked at 900,000 EUR.
€ Funds
18,000–45,000 EUR option premium → Landowners. No other outlay.
Step 2 · October
Trial Event
⚖ Legal
NCA operates the trial event on the Huesca site under the lease right granted by the option agreement.
€ Funds
Event operating costs only. No land payment.
Step 3 · Post-October
GO / NO-GO Vote
⚖ Legal
Community votes with real ground truth from October. Board decides based on mandate strength and pledge status.
€ Funds
No movement. Decision point only.
YES → Continue to Steps 4 and 5 NO → Let option expire. See failure path below.
Step 4 · Now → Post-Vote
Formalize Pledges
⚖ Legal
Each pledger signs a Préstamo (loan, repaid after closing) or Donación (gift, no repayment).
€ Funds
900,000 EUR committed as signed legal instruments.
Step 5 · Closing
NCA Buys Land
⚖ Legal
Option exercised. Contrato de Compraventa signed. Land Registry transfer. NCA holds clean title.
€ Funds
900,000 EUR → Landowners
72,000 EUR ITP → Tax Authority
If vote fails ↓

Option Expires — NCA Walks

Total loss: 18,000–45,000 EUR in option premium only. No land commitment. No investor exposure. Clean exit.

This is the designed cost of optionality — not a failure mode. It is the price of the right to buy without being committed to buying.

ITP (Impuesto de Transmisiones Patrimoniales) · Aragón rate 8% · 8% × 900,000 EUR = 72,000 EUR paid by NCA as buyer at closing
