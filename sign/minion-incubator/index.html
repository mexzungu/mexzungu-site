Dear James,

Thank you for the opportunity to support Minion Incubator Ltd. This letter sets out the terms on which Mexzungu Group Ltd will provide services to you. The engagement will be led by Faith Gathoni, Practice Lead for Governance and Founder Development.

Scope of Work

WS1 (Governance Architecture) + WS2 (Legal Advisory Retainer). Full details at mexzungu.com/proposals/minion-incubator.

Fees

WS1: USD 2,500 fixed fee (50% on signing, 50% on Governance Handbook delivery). WS2: USD 2,000 per month, invoiced monthly in advance. Minimum two months on WS2. Payment by bank transfer or Wise.

Term and Termination

WS1 commences on receipt of the first payment and concludes on delivery of the Governance Handbook, estimated 8 to 12 weeks from kick-off. WS2 continues on a rolling monthly basis. Either party may terminate WS2 with 30 days written notice. WS1 fees are non-refundable once work has commenced.

Nature of Services

All services are advisory and structural in nature. Mexzungu Group Ltd is not licensed to practice Kenyan law and does not provide Kenyan legal advice. Nothing in this engagement creates a solicitor-client relationship under the Law Society of Kenya. For matters requiring admitted Kenyan counsel, we will provide referrals to trusted practitioners in Nairobi.

Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep the terms of this engagement and all information exchanged in the course of the work strictly confidential. This obligation survives termination of the engagement.

Governing Law

This letter is governed by the laws of Mauritius. Any dispute that cannot be resolved amicably will be referred to arbitration under the rules of the Mauritius Chamber of Commerce and Industry.
