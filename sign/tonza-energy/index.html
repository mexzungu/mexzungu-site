Parties

Mexzungu Group Ltd, incorporated in Mauritius, represented by Jose Pepe Carrillo Perez ("Advisor").

Stephen Khan, Co-Founder of Tonza Energy ("Client").

Recitals

Client is co-founding Tonza Energy, a Kenya-based clean cooking hardware company pivoting to smart connected products, and is seeking advisory support to build the legal foundation for a pre-seed fundraise and receive deal review and closing support as investor conversations progress.

Scope of Services

Workstream 1: Venture Architecture

  • Entity review and structuring: Kenyan entity audit (or incorporation guidance if pending), confirm the right structure for international impact investors, assess whether a holding company layer is needed for foreign capital compatibility.
  • Cap table architecture: Founder allocation, vesting schedule, option pool sizing for future hires and advisors, pre-money structure ready for the first SAFE or priced seed round.
  • Founder and shareholder agreements: Founder rights, drag-along and tag-along, right of first refusal, anti-dilution framework, consent matters. Built for Kenya but legible to international impact investors.
  • IP assignment and smart product framework: All current and future IP (technology, firmware, sensor data, brand, designs) formally assigned from founders to the company. Critical before sharing anything with a hardware partner or new hire on the smart product pivot.
  • SAFE template and governance framework: Investor-ready SAFE document, board composition, decision rights, officer roles.

Workstream 2: Deal Review & Closing Support

  • Pitch materials review and narrative sharpening: Strategic input on positioning for investor conversations already underway. Lead with Kenya market gap, smart product differentiation, and carbon credit angle.
  • Carbon credit pathway assessment: Kenya clean cooking generates high-quality verified carbon credits under Gold Standard and Verra. Assessment of whether the product design qualifies and what that changes in unit economics and investor narrative.
  • Term sheet review and negotiation advisory: Once Tonza has investor conversations in progress, review incoming term sheets and advise on key terms. Protect founders on valuation, dilution, control, and information rights. Kenya-calibrated, international-investor legible.
  • Closing coordination: SAFE or priced round documentation review, investor onboarding, executed documentation support.

Exclusions: This engagement does not include Kenya domestic legal advice (Pepe Carrillo is not licensed to practice law in Kenya), direct engagement with government bodies, or operations hiring (advisory only on team structuring). For local filings and regulatory submissions, Mexzungu works alongside a Kenya-licensed law firm and will make introductions at no additional cost.

Important: Scope Clarification

Pepe Carrillo is not a fundraising agent and does not source or introduce investors. This engagement covers deal review, term negotiation, and closing support. It applies once Tonza has investor conversations in progress. Mexzungu does not prospect on Tonza's behalf, operate as a placement agent, or make investor introductions as part of this engagement. Client is responsible for generating investor interest and initiating conversations.
