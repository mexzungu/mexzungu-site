Why does this letter exist?

Because a good working relationship deserves a clear written foundation. We talked about the kind of work each of us does, liked what we saw, and agreed to explore whether we can source and deliver governance and founder development mandates together. This letter captures the framework for that collaboration: what it means to work together, how fees flow, what we each own, and how to exit cleanly if it stops making sense.

1. What kind of relationship is this?

Faith is an independent advisor, not an employee of Mexzungu Group Ltd. Nothing in this letter creates an employment relationship, a partnership, or a joint venture. Faith chooses which mandates to accept, sets the methodology for delivery, and operates her own practice independently. Mexzungu provides the platform, the pipeline, and the infrastructure. Faith provides the expertise.

2. What Faith brings to the collaboration

Governance and founder development expertise. Faith brings her full practice: governance architecture, founder development facilitation, and the Sacred Work Series across the wellness and apothecary space. She sets the methodology, the approach, and the delivery standards.
Her own client relationships. Faith retains full ownership of existing client relationships and any practice she has built independently. Mexzungu makes no claim over those.
Professional judgment. Faith decides whether a referred mandate is a good fit and has the right to decline any mandate without explanation.

3. What Mexzungu provides

Client sourcing and BD. Mexzungu will use commercially reasonable efforts to identify and introduce governance and founder development mandates that fit Faith's practice. There is no guaranteed volume or minimum number of referrals. Pipeline development takes time and depends on market conditions, network activity, and client needs.
Platform and legal infrastructure. Access to the Mexzungu platform for proposals, contracts, and billing. The Studio manages client onboarding, engagement letters, and invoice administration.
Operational support. Access to Mexzungu's operational layer, including scheduling, communications infrastructure, and coordination support where relevant to a shared mandate.

4. How fees work

Revenue on mandates sourced through the Mexzungu platform is split 80/20. Faith keeps 80% of all fees billed on mandates she delivers. Mexzungu retains 20% to cover client sourcing, business development, legal infrastructure, and operational support.

Faith's published fee schedule at the time of signing is set out below. Fees on any given mandate are agreed in writing before delivery begins and are subject to client negotiation. The schedule is a reference framework, not a guarantee of the fees that will be achieved on any specific mandate.

Engagement type Published fee Faith keeps (80%)
Discovery Call $350 $280
Founder Development Intensive $3,500 $2,800
Governance Architecture $6,500 to $9,500 $5,200 to $7,600
Monthly Retainer $2,500 / mo $2,000 / mo

The 80/20 split applies to all mandates sourced through Mexzungu regardless of engagement type. For mandates Faith sources independently through her own practice, no fee share applies unless she explicitly routes them through the platform. Fee schedule may be updated by written agreement between the parties.

5. Payment and invoicing

Mexzungu issues the client invoice and collects payment. Faith's 80% is remitted within 14 days of cleared client payment. Mexzungu is not required to remit Faith's share before client payment clears. If a client does not pay, no fee is owed to Faith for that mandate.

6. What Mexzungu is not doing

(a) Guaranteeing any volume of mandates, referrals, or income at any level.
(b) Employing Faith or creating any right to benefits, holiday pay, or employment protections.
(c) Restricting Faith from working independently or with other platforms and clients.
(d) Taking responsibility for the quality, accuracy, or outcomes of Faith's advisory work.

7. Intellectual property

Faith retains full ownership of all methodologies, frameworks, tools, and materials she has developed independently, including the Sacred Work Series, her governance frameworks, and any pre-existing materials. Where a mandate results in new joint materials specifically developed for a client, ownership is addressed in the specific engagement letter for that mandate.

8. Confidentiality

Both parties treat each other's business information, client details, methodologies, and pricing as confidential. Neither party shares the other's materials, client names, or internal information without written permission. This obligation survives the end of this letter.

9. How long does this last?

This framework is open-ended. Either party may end it at any time with 30 days written notice. Any mandate already in progress continues until completed, and the 80/20 split applies to fees collected from that mandate. Confidentiality survives termination.

10. Governing law

Mauritius law. Any disputes that cannot be resolved between the parties go to the Supreme Court of Mauritius (Commercial Division). We are not expecting to need this.
