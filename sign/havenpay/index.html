Why does this letter exist?

Because handshakes are beautiful and engagement letters are how the universe remembers them. We talked about the architecture, liked what we saw, and agreed on the shape of the work. This letter captures that shape: what we are doing, what we are not, what it costs, and how we treat each other if anything ever wobbles. Read it, sign it, and get to work.

Where does the Studio sit, geographically and legally?

The Studio is a Mauritius outfit. We work out of our registered office on the island and deliver from there to wherever HavenPay happens to be operating. Pepe signs as the Studio Chief Disruption Officer. None of what follows means we have set up shop, opened a branch, or started practising law in Kenya, Uganda, Delaware, or anywhere else. We are the visiting architects, not the local lawyers.

1. What are we working on together?

HavenPay is building two things simultaneously. HavenPay Foundation receives grant capital from DFIs, foundations, and UNHCR to fund the Refugee Financial Identity Stack and community infrastructure. HavenPay Technologies owns the platform, IP, and the Choice Bank BaaS relationship, holds the cap table, and generates revenue. The dual-entity model is the right architecture. The problem is that the architecture in your head is not yet the same as a legally defensible, investor-ready structure. That gap is what we close.

This engagement covers two workstreams. You activate the ones you need. Both are defined below. The workstreams you are signing up for are confirmed in the activation section before your signature.

Workstream 1: Venture Architecture. A full architecture blueprint covering entity structure, governance, IP licensing framework, fundraising architecture, and investor narrative. Four decision-ready deliverables, delivered within 4 weeks of engagement start.
Workstream 2: Deal Closing. We are in the room with you on the capital raise. First investor call, term sheet negotiation, and closing call, always. Available on 48 hours notice for anything else that surfaces before money clears.

2. What do you actually walk away with?

Workstream 1 only. Each deliverable is a decision-ready document, not a deck.

Entity Architecture Memo. Confirmed entity map (Foundation jurisdiction analysis + Technologies HoldCo jurisdiction recommendation — Delaware vs Mauritius vs BVI), governance structure for each entity, which entity employs who, owns what, and receives what, with specific reference to the Choice Bank BaaS relationship, and regulatory requirements across Kenya, Uganda, and the HoldCo jurisdiction.
IP Licensing Framework. Full IP asset inventory covering HavenScore, the Refugee Financial Identity Stack, transaction data protocols, identity verification methodology, platform technology, and brand. Licensing terms defensible to a DFI grant officer and a Series A investor simultaneously. Carve-outs for open-access grant deliverables.
Fundraising Architecture. Capital flow diagram, grant receipt pathway, pre-seed instrument recommendation (SAFE vs convertible note vs priced round), cap table design, and DFI debt facility pathway.
Investor Narrative Brief. One page. Three audiences: a Systems Disruptor family office, a DFI or foundation grant officer, a future Series A institutional investor.

3. What are we assuming going in?

We are building this engagement on a few working assumptions. If anything shifts materially, we talk and adjust.

(a) IP is not yet formally assigned. We assume the relevant IP assets have not yet been formally vested in any entity. Phase 1 will map what exists and how to vest it cleanly. If prior assignments have been made, share them and we adjust the scope accordingly.
(b) What we need from you. When we ask for existing documents, term sheets, investor materials, or corporate records, we need them within 5 business days. If things come in late, the clock adjusts accordingly.
(c) Governing entity. HavenPay Technologies as a formal legal entity may not yet be incorporated at the time of signing. This engagement is entered into between Mexzungu Group Ltd and Yoeal Haile, with the understanding that Phase 1 deliverables will inform the final entity selection and formation in Phase 2.

4. What are we not doing?

(a) Filing anything with any court, registry, notary, or government office, anywhere.
(b) Being your lawyer of record in Kenya, Uganda, Delaware, or anywhere else.
(c) Representing you in front of investors or counterparties unless you give us a written mandate to do so.
(d) Replacing your local lawyers. For execution in your chosen jurisdictions, you will need qualified local counsel. We can point you toward good people.
(e) Anything that is not in sections 1 and 2 above.

5. Is this legal advice?

No. This is legal consulting. We give you the architecture, the risks, and the playbook. We do not file papers, sign legal opinions, or appear in court. Think of us as the friend who has closed a few billion in deals across a bunch of countries, sitting next to you at the table, telling you what to look out for.

6. What does this cost?

Administration fee — billed monthly from day one. While any workstream is open, a monthly admin fee is invoiced automatically: $500 per month for one active workstream, $900 per month for two. This is not a retainer or a discretionary line item. It is how the engagement is administered and invoiced. It stops the day the last active workstream closes.
Workstream 2 success fee — payable when capital clears. 2% of equity raised, or 1% of any debt facility. Payable within 14 days of money clearing. The admin fee runs in parallel throughout — the success fee is the additional charge triggered by the outcome. Applies to capital closed within 12 months of engagement start. If the engagement ends early and a deal closes within 6 months with a party we worked with during the engagement, the success fee still applies.

8. What happens if something goes wrong?

If something goes sideways and you come after us, the most we are on the hook for is the cash you have actually paid us at that point. Not hypothetical future fees, not consequential losses. The two things we cannot contractually cap are fraud and willful misconduct. We are not planning on either.

9. Who else sees what we discuss?

Nobody. What happens in this engagement stays in this engagement. You do not share our memos, analysis, or thinking outside this engagement without our permission. We do not share your business, structure, or strategy with anyone. This commitment does not expire when the engagement does.

10. Can the Studio talk about this work later?

Yes, in general terms. Once everything is public or done, we might say something like "we helped a refugee fintech build the architecture for a dual-entity structure and capital raise." We will never name HavenPay, share your documents, or say anything specific without your written permission.

11. How long does this last?

We start on the date at the top and keep going until one of these happens first: (a) All active workstreams are complete and all fees are settled. (b) Twelve months have passed. (c) One of us tells the other in writing that we are done, for any reason or no reason, with 14 days notice. Individual workstreams can be closed independently by written agreement without ending the full engagement. If we stop early, everything delivered stays delivered. Everything earned stays earned. Confidentiality survives.

12. Which law applies if we ever disagree?

Mauritius law. If we cannot sort it out between ourselves, it goes to the Supreme Court of Mauritius (Commercial Division). We are not expecting to need this. It is here so neither of us ever has to wonder.

13. What are the service standards?

Our response times, call availability, deliverable revision policy, and mutual obligations are published at mexzungu.com/sla. That document is incorporated into this engagement by reference. If we update it materially, we will notify you in writing before the change takes effect.
