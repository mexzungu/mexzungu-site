Why does this letter exist?

Nick and Lyndsay Handler own and operate Saranac Lodge, a luxury eco-lodge in Uganda. You are in advanced discussions to sell a 75% stake to an overseas buyer (Woo) for $900,000. The deal requires the right entity structure, clean documentation, and a clear path from handshake to signed purchase agreement. You have asked us to structure the transaction and support the negotiation through to close. This letter captures what we are doing, what it costs, and how we treat each other along the way.

1. What are we working on together?

Three phases, sequential:

Phase 1: Delaware LLC Formation. We design and document the legal architecture for Saranac USA LLC, the vehicle through which Woo will hold its 75% economic interest. This covers: constitutional documents, operating agreement, equity structure, and coordination with the registered agent on formation. Woo's investment flows into the US entity. Land title and lodge operations remain in Uganda.
Phase 2: Deal Documentation. We draft and negotiate the primary transaction documents: (a) Membership Interest Purchase Agreement governing Woo's acquisition of 75% of Saranac USA LLC; (b) the framework linking Uganda lodge operations and assets to the US entity; (c) the post-close arrangement defining Nick's ongoing position and compensation. We coordinate with your Ugandan counsel on the local law elements.
Phase 3: Transaction Support through Close. We are in your corner throughout: reviewing and redlining any documents from Woo's side within 48 hours of receipt, advising on negotiation positions, coordinating with your Ugandan counsel on the asset assignment, and staying with you until the purchase agreement is signed.

2. What are we not doing?

(a) Filing anything with any court, registry, notary, or government office in Uganda, the United States, or anywhere else.
(b) Acting as your lawyer of record in any jurisdiction.
(c) Providing licensed legal advice in Uganda or the United States.
(d) Replacing your Ugandan counsel. Local filings, title transfers, and in-country execution require qualified local lawyers. We coordinate with them; we do not replace them.
(e) Anything outside sections 1 above.

5. How long does this last?

We start on the date above and continue until whichever comes first: (a) the purchase agreement is signed and the transaction closes; (b) 12 months have passed; or (c) one of us tells the other in writing that we are done, with 14 days notice, for any reason. Work delivered stays delivered. Fees earned stay earned. Confidentiality survives termination.

6. Which law applies?

Mauritius law governs this letter. If we cannot resolve a dispute between ourselves, it goes to the Supreme Court of Mauritius (Commercial Division). We are not planning to need this.

7. Confidentiality

What happens in this engagement stays in this engagement. We do not share your business details, deal terms, or strategy with anyone outside this engagement without your written permission. You do not share our analysis, memos, or thinking without ours. This commitment does not expire when the engagement does. Once the transaction is public, we may reference it in general terms (deal type, geography, structure) without naming Saranac Ventures or any party, unless you give us written permission to do so.

8. Non-solicitation

During this engagement and for 12 months after it ends, neither party will directly solicit the other's key personnel, contractors, or advisors for employment or engagement. This does not restrict either party from responding to public job postings or general solicitations.

9. Limitation of liability

If something goes wrong and you come after us, our maximum liability is the cash fees actually paid under this letter at that point. We are not on the hook for lost profits, consequential losses, or anything not directly caused by our own conduct. The two things we cannot contractually limit are fraud and willful misconduct. We are not planning on either.
