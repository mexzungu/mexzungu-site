Why does this letter exist?

Because good work needs a clear foundation. You run a $20M fund across 30+ African countries with 16 portfolio companies and the structural and strategic complexity that comes with that. This letter sets out what we are doing together, what it costs, and how we operate. Read it, sign it, and let's get to work.

Where does the Studio sit, legally and operationally?

The Studio is a Mauritius outfit. We work from our registered office on the island and deliver across borders. None of what follows means we have opened a branch or started practising local law anywhere in Africa. We are capital and venture structuring consultants. The visiting architects, not the local lawyers.

1. What are we working on together?

The Studio Foundation Retainer gives Delta40 ongoing access to Mexzungu's venture architecture and capital structuring expertise across four pillars:

Fund governance and legal strategy. Term sheet reviews, LP agreement analysis, governance architecture, and structural decisions at fund level. You get a deal-seasoned eye on everything before it moves.
Portfolio company support. Term sheets, shareholder agreements, board governance, founder equity structures, and commercial contract reviews across the portfolio. Response within 48 hours on anything flagged as urgent.
Founder education and coaching. Workshops, deal clinics, and one-on-one sessions for portfolio founders on structuring, capital raises, and commercial fundamentals. We help your portfolio not make the mistakes that cost them later.
Transaction advisory. For portfolio companies in active capital raise or M&A processes, we provide structuring and negotiation support through to close. Success fees apply when a deal closes (see Section 6).

2. What do you actually walk away with?

(a) Structured review and annotated positions on every material term sheet or agreement you send us, within 48 hours.
(b) A plain-language memo on what to hold, what to give, and where the walkaway is, for any deal where you ask for one.
(c) Access to Pepe's network across legal, capital, and institutional investor communities in Africa, Europe, and beyond.
(d) A direct line, not a ticketing queue. You reach Pepe. That is the deal.

3. What are we not doing?

(a) Filing anything with any court, registry, notary, or government office anywhere.
(b) Being the lawyer of record for Delta40 or any portfolio company in any jurisdiction.
(c) Regulatory compliance monitoring or filing coordination. This is not a law firm engagement.
(d) Replacing local counsel. For execution in specific jurisdictions, local qualified lawyers are required. We can point you toward good people.
(e) Anything not described in Section 1.

4. Is this legal advice?

No. This is legal consulting and venture architecture. We give you the structure, the risks, and the playbook. Think of us as the friend who has closed a few billion in deals across a dozen countries, sitting next to you at the table, telling you what to look out for and how to build things that hold.

5. Assumptions

(a) Coverage scope: The retainer covers Delta40 at fund level and all portfolio companies active as at the signing date, up to the tier limit below. New portfolio companies are included automatically within the same tier limit.
(b) If we part ways early: If either side ends the engagement and a transaction closes within 6 months with someone we introduced or worked with during the engagement, the applicable success fee still applies.
(c) What we need from you: When we ask for documents, term sheets, cap tables, or financials, we need them within 5 business days. If things come in late, timelines adjust accordingly.

6. What does this cost?

The retainer fee is structured by portfolio size and billed monthly:

Portfolio size Monthly fee Notes
Up to 20 companies Current $5,500/mo Full fund-level and portfolio coverage
21 to 35 companies $7,000/mo Triggers automatically on portfolio growth
36+ companies $8,500/mo Cap; no further scaling above this

Fees are exclusive of registration fees and local counsel fees (if any). Tier upgrades take effect on the first day of the month following the trigger event. Pepe will flag the trigger before invoicing.

Hour Bank. For work outside the retainer scope (specialist projects, extended transaction advisory, bespoke research), Delta40 may pre-purchase blocks of 20 hours at $200/hr. Unused hours carry forward to the following month. Standard rate outside blocks is $250/hr.
Transaction success fees. When a portfolio company closes a capital raise with Mexzungu in an active advisory role: equity closes at 1.5% of total equity raised, debt closes at 1% of total facility. Retainer clients receive a discount on the standard success fee rates. Success fees are payable within 14 days of capital clearing. Fees under the retainer credit mechanism reduce the first success fee invoice pro-rata for months of active coverage prior to close.

8. Liability

If something goes wrong and you come after us, the most we are on the hook for is the retainer fees you have actually paid in the preceding 3 months. Not consequential losses, not advisory value foregone, not hypothetical future savings. Fraud and willful misconduct are not capped. We are not planning on either.

9. Confidentiality

What happens in this engagement stays in this engagement. You do not share our memos, analysis, or thinking outside this engagement without our permission. We do not share your fund strategy, portfolio details, or any company information with anyone. This commitment does not expire when the engagement does.

10. Can the Studio reference this work later?

Yes, in general terms only. We might say something like "we provide ongoing venture architecture support to an African-focused VC fund." We will never name Delta40, share your portfolio details, or say anything specific without your written permission. Your call whether to name us on the wall.

11. Duration

This engagement starts on the date at the top and continues until one of these happens: (a) Either side gives 30 days written notice of termination, for any reason. (b) Twelve months pass, at which point we agree a renewal or part ways cleanly. Everything delivered stays delivered. Everything earned stays earned. Confidentiality survives.

12. Governing law

Mauritius law. If we cannot sort it out between ourselves, it goes to the Supreme Court of Mauritius (Commercial Division). We are not expecting to need this.
