Why does this letter exist?

You have a device that works and a structure that does not fit what this is becoming. This letter captures what we are going to build together: the entity, the IP transfer, the cap table, and the fundraising architecture. Sign it and we start.

1. What are we working on?

Audacity Labs has developed an EV charging safety device. The intellectual property and development history sit in Audacity Labs (Ghana), but that entity is not the right vehicle for raising capital, holding IP across jurisdictions, or building a proper cap table. The goal of this engagement is to build the right structure from the ground up before you go near an investor.

Five deliverables:

Entity Architecture Memo. New HoldCo structure, jurisdiction recommendation, board composition, and decision rights. The blueprint everything else follows from.
IP Assignment Agreement. Formal legal transfer of all intellectual property from Audacity Labs to the new entity. Covers the Goliath engineering contract, monitoring software, brand, and any patents in process. Clean chain of title before any investor sees the cap table.
Cap Table Design. Founder shares, vesting schedule, and option pool if needed. Built to survive a term sheet without surprises.
Fundraising Instrument Design. Recommendation on SAFE, priced equity, or convertible note for the initial raise. Instrument drafted and ready to issue.
Investor Narrative Brief. One page. Written for the investor conversation. The questions they will ask about your structure are the ones this document answers before they ask them.

2. What this does not include

Any advice on commercial partnerships or third-party arrangements. That is a separate engagement if and when it is needed.
Dispute resolution or enforcement claims against Goliath or any prior contractor. We are building forward.
Local Ghanaian or Rwandan filing and registration. We coordinate with local counsel. Their fees are yours directly, at cost.
Incorporation fees and government filing costs. Billed to you at cost, no markup, separately invoiced.
US tax advice. We flag the right questions and point you to the right CPA.

3. Assumptions going in

Jurisdiction. The choice of entity jurisdiction (Delaware, European HoldCo, or other) will be determined together once we know where the majority of your investment capital is likely to come from. We will make a recommendation. You make the final call.
Timeline. Target: entity formation complete within 3 months of signing. If external factors push this out, we adjust the milestone payment accordingly.
What we need from you. Goliath contract, all development receipts, and any existing Audacity Labs corporate documents, within 5 business days of signing.
