What you get
Not a summary. A structured review.

Risk score

0 to 100 across five dimensions: structure, investor protections, financial transparency, exit path, and process conduct.

Terms extracted

What you own, what you paid for it, what rights you have, and what the documents are silent on. Silence is flagged, not ignored.

Anything that should stop or slow you down. Wire-before-docs clauses. Missing anti-dilution. Unexplained valuations. Named explicitly.
