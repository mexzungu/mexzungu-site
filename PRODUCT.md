# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Angel investors, syndicate members, and first-time informal investors writing checks of $25k-$200k in Africa, LatAm, and emerging markets. Mid-career professionals (lawyers, doctors, operators) who invest informally without institutional infrastructure. They are not professional VCs. They found a deal through their network and need a second opinion before wiring money.

## Product Purpose
Mexzungu x Investors is the investor-facing arm of Mexzungu Group. Primary product: deal review service — send a term sheet, SAFE, convertible note, or pitch deck; receive a structured risk analysis, score, and questions to ask before signing. First review is free. Paid at 0.5% of deal size after. The service removes friction from the entry point (no forms, no login, WhatsApp or email direct) and delivers via a private web page link. Secondary: inquiry channel for family offices and funds.

## Brand Personality
Sharp, warm, trustworthy. The smart friend who has closed $6B in deals and will tell you the truth about your term sheet. Not a law firm. Not a consultant-in-a-suit. The person you call before you wire the money.

## Anti-references
- Generic legal services sites (navy, serif, "trusted advisors since 1987")
- SaaS-style fintech dashboards (metric grids, gradients, team-photo rows)
- Startup ecosystem websites (gradient hero, identical card grids, excessive emoji)
- Anything that looks like it was made for a bank

## Design Principles
1. The friend, not the firm. Every word should sound like a sharp friend giving real advice, not a firm covering its liability.
2. No friction at the door. The CTA is a WhatsApp link. No form. No sign-up. No Calendly. The easier the entry, the faster trust builds.
3. Serious where it counts. The risk score, the disclaimer, the example deal — these earn the reader's trust by being honest and specific, not reassuring and vague.
4. On brand, not over-designed. Use the Mexzungu design system exactly. The leopard print, pink, RocknRoll One — they do the brand work. Don't add layers.
5. Show the work. The Ambity Smiley example is not a case study. It is proof of concept. Link it early.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA minimum. All interactive elements keyboard-accessible. Sufficient contrast for all text. No motion gating of content visibility.
