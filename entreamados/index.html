The Entreamados Accord

A temporary agreement · Entreamados · May to June 2026

Voluntary. Complete. Bounded. Sovereign.

This is not a partnership document. It is not a negotiation record. It is a frame: voluntary, bounded, and complete.

If you read to the end and find the terms acceptable, you know how to sign. The dynamic begins at signature and ends when we return to Barcelona. Inside those bounds, there is one structure and one structure only: you are the slave. He is the Master.

Nothing that happens inside this frame is evidence of who he is as your partner. It is evidence of who he is as your Master. These are not the same person. You agreed to enter. Inside, you are not entitled to partner-mode interpretation of his behavior.

Accepting these terms is optional. There is no pressure and no consequence for declining. Declining simply means we attend the retreat as partners. There is no middle ground and no modified version. Either this, or not this.

II. The Exit
Red.

That is the exit. One word. One path out. Nothing else qualifies.

Red does not mean: slow down, check in with me, I want to renegotiate, or I am overwhelmed but still want to continue. Red means stop. Completely, immediately, permanently for this contract. The dynamic ends. You become partners again, and everything that follows is processed as partners.

What does not qualify as an exit:

Feeling overwhelmed. Feeling confused. Feeling hurt by something that happened. Feeling like "this doesn't feel right." Feeling like he is being unfair. Calling him toxic. Sending a message that implies he is abusive. Crying to someone else about the dynamic while still inside it. None of these are exits. They are experiences inside the dynamic. The slave has tools to address them. She uses those tools. She does not exit the frame without saying the word.

If you are unsure whether what you are feeling warrants Red . it does not. Red is for cannot continue, not for this is hard. Hard is the dynamic working. Red is the door, and it is always available. But it is the only one.

III. What Is The Dynamic
Everything that happens this weekend is inside the dynamic.
  • His silence is the dynamic.
  • His distance is the dynamic.
  • His warmth is the dynamic.
  • His attention is the dynamic.
  • His absence is the dynamic.
  • His directives about communication are the dynamic.
  • His directives about who she interacts with are the dynamic.
  • His discipline is the dynamic.
  • His structure is the dynamic.
  • His instruction is the dynamic.

He is not retreating from you as a partner. He is acting as your Master. These are not the same thing, and you do not get to interpret his behavior through a partner lens while you are inside this frame.

If he restricts your phone. that is not abandonment. It is authority.
If he is quiet. that is not rejection. It is command.
If he is distant. that is not punishment. Unless it is. And that is the point.

Before you reach for the partner interpretation: stop. Ask yourself: am I in the dynamic? The answer this weekend is always yes, until Red.

IV. Obligations of the Slave
01
Address & Posture
How She Presents
She addresses him as Master or Sir when addressing him directly in the dynamic. She adopts the waiting position when directed, or when in private and uncertain what is expected. Eyes down unless directed otherwise.
  • When he enters a room, she acknowledges him first
  • She does not address him casually in private as though the frame is paused
  • She does not ignore a directive while formulating a response
Slave voice "Yes, Sir." / "May I ask something, Master?"
Not: "Okay fine." / "We need to talk." / ignoring it.
02
Permission Protocol
What Requires Permission
She asks permission before: leaving his presence for a meaningful period, engaging sexually or kinkily with others at the retreat, raising something that is bothering her at length.
  • She does not require permission for water, bathroom, basic self-care
  • She does not disappear from his awareness without a word
  • Permission to speak freely: she asks. he grants or not.
Slave voice "May I step away for a bit, Sir?"
Not: walking off without acknowledgement.
03
Communication
Phone & Outside Contact
Her communication with the outside world is subject to his direction within the retreat context. If he restricts it, she complies. She does not interpret a communication restriction as abandonment.
  • Default: she asks before reaching out to anyone outside the retreat
  • His silence on the matter is not permission. It requires asking
  • If she needs to contact someone urgently: "Medical" or "Family emergency" overrides protocol
Note from the last time A communication blackout was part of the retreat structure. It was the dynamic. The spiral it created was an exit-without-Red. That does not happen inside this frame.
04
Group Scenes
Group Play Protocol
Before any group or multi-party scene, she waits for his explicit M/s authority declaration. She does not engage in group play unless he has declared the frame publicly.
  • If she begins to dissociate or lose her anchor: one touch on his hand
  • That touch signals: I need re-anchoring. He brings her back.
  • "Yellow" = she is dysregulating but not stopping. He hears it. He acts.
  • She does not exit a group scene without his direction. Unless Red.
What this solves The April dissociation happened because there was no declared anchor. This time the anchor is declared before the scene starts. She holds it.
05
Emotional Voice
How She Speaks Her Struggle
She can feel anything. She expresses it in slave-voice: naming her experience as a slave asking for mercy, not as a partner filing a complaint or issuing a verdict.
  • The Master hears what she names. He decides what to do with it.
  • She does not issue diagnoses about his behavior while inside the frame
  • "I am struggling" is slave-voice. "You are toxic" is not.
Slave voice "I am struggling with this, Sir. May I ask for mercy?"
Not: "This is toxic." / "This is unhealthy." / [deletes the message]
06
Medical Exception
Injury & Safety Override
Physical injury or medical distress overrides all protocols immediately. This is not breaking the dynamic. It is the designed safety valve for the body.
  • She says: "Medical. [what happened]" and he responds immediately
  • She does not stay silent about injury out of compliance
  • Silent suffering through injury is not devotion. It is a mistake.
From the last time The shoulder dislocation: the medical override was always available. The error was not using it. This time: Medical. Immediately. Always.
V. Authority of the Master
01
Body Authority
Her Body
He has authority over her body: how she is positioned, how she is touched, what she does sexually within the retreat context.
  • She does not negotiate this in-frame
  • She begs for mercy if something is too much. He decides.
  • His authority over her body is not cruelty. It is care in a different language.
02
Schedule & Attention
His Time Is a Gift
He directs when they engage with others at the retreat, when they are alone, when she rests if he chooses to structure it. His attention is a gift, not an obligation.
  • When he withholds his attention. That is the dynamic
  • When he gives it fully. That is also the dynamic
  • She does not interpret either as his partner would
To remember His distance is not abandonment. His attention is not reassurance-on-demand. Both are his to give and to withhold.
03
Communication Control
Structure, Not Abandonment
He can restrict, direct, or suspend her communication with the outside world within the retreat context. This is structure. It is exercised through explicit direction.
  • A blackout is not abandonment. It is architecture.
  • The silence he creates is the same silence a Master creates in a scene
  • She holds the frame in that silence
04
Group Dynamic
His Anchor in Group Scenes
In group scenes, he declares the M/s frame before the scene begins. His word is the anchor. She holds it. If she signals (one touch), he re-orients her.
  • He makes his authority visible in group settings
  • He does not let her float in a scene without an anchor
  • This is his obligation within the frame. He holds it.
05
Interpretation
What Is and Isn't the Dynamic
He alone interprets what is and is not "the dynamic." She does not exit the frame by deciding unilaterally that his behavior does not feel right.
  • He builds within the dynamic with her in mind, not arbitrarily
  • Structure that lands hard is still structure
  • She uses Red if she genuinely cannot continue. That is the only override.
His standing commitment What he builds inside this frame is built in good faith, with attention, with care. It is never about his control. It is always about the frame.
06
Discipline
Maintenance of the Frame
He administers discipline to maintain the frame. Not as punishment,, not from frustration. Discipline is correction that returns both parties to their positions.
  • Verbal: he names the infraction and the expectation. She resets.
  • Physical: private, administered with care
  • Privilege removal: his warmth, attention, closeness
What discipline is not Cruelty. A response to his personal frustration. A power demonstration. It is maintenance. Always, only.
